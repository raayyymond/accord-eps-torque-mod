## 🛑 THE PRE-REGISTERED FALSIFIER FIRED

V83a was built to move the **26–31 Hz ring** by changing the damper dose. The ring came back at
**1.021 — dead flat** — while the two bands the dose was *not* aimed at both got substantially worse.

⇒ **THE DAMPER-DOSE MODEL OF THE 26–31 Hz RING IS FALSIFIED.** It is not a knob the damper dose
reaches. This is the second symptom to come out dose-independent, after grind #1
([[accord-grind1-is-inert-to-the-damper-dose]]) — and unlike that one, **V83a declared the falsifier
before the flight**, so the null costs nothing in interpretation.

⚠ V83a is not a clean single-variable damper test either: it **reverted mode 26 but left mode 27
carrying V81's relay damper**, which is a live engaged column — see
[[accord-mode-27-is-a-second-engaged-column]]. The 18–22 Hz and 6–9 Hz regressions are therefore
attributable to *"an engaged relay damper is still on the car, on the other column"* at least as
readily as to the mode-26 revert.
