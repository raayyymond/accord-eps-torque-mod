★★★★ **r24 IS THE GRIND-#1 ACTOR. r26 IS NEARLY BLIND TO IT.** 2026-08-08.

The kit carried an unexplained tension for four sessions: **V62/V65 raised r26 ×2 and helped; V67/V68 cut
r26 ÷6 and helped more.** Two opposite moves on the same lane, both scored as wins. **It is resolved, and
the resolution is that neither r26 move did anything** — both builds also moved **r24**, in the same
direction, and r24 is what grind #1 follows.
