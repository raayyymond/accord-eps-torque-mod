## What the prior handoffs got WRONG (both fixed in V29)

1. **The V28 model ("wall = max(driver-torque, corridor)") was incomplete.** Reading the shaper
   `FUN_00042af8` decompile (lines ~582–644) shows the int wall is a **THREE-WAY MAX**:
   ```
   gp-0x6af6 = max( dir_corridor[cal 0x774e] , driver-torque IIR[gp-0x3574>>8] , boost[cal 0x7760] ) × polarity
   ```
   - The **direction corridor** (`0x774e`, the soft-EME table, velocity-indexed flat ±1024) IS one arm —
     that is why V25 (doubling `0x774e`) desynced the monitor.
   - The **boost** table (`0x7760` int = X[700,800,1100] Y[0,1536,2048]; float mirror `0xC65B8` Y[0,1.5,2.0])
     is a *separate* arm (Tracer B's "r23" — NOT the corridor, as V28 had assumed).
   - Both monitors (shaper Monitor 1 @ line 642 / `~0x43190`; watchdog Monitor 2 `FUN_00043e44` @ `0x4463a`)
     cross-check the **float twins `gp-0x6db0`/`gp-0x6db8` (×1024) vs the int walls `gp-0x6af6`/`gp-0x6b00`**,
     window ±5 LSB → ≥128.0 → `FUN_000462e6(0x3f1b)` hard shutdown (DTC `0xF00049`).

2. **The handoff's proposed V29 float address `0xC6590`/`0xC65A4` was WRONG** — those are the **X (velocity)
   breakpoints** (−8.0 / +1.0), not the corridor magnitude. **The correct float corridor-mirror Y-values are
   `0xC6598`/`0xC659C` (dir1, +1.0) and `0xC65AC`/`0xC65B0` (dir2, −1.0)** — verified from the stock bytes AND
   from the watchdog disasm (`movea 0x7598,tp,r12` dir1 / `movea 0x75ac,tp,r14` dir2 are the LERP Y bases).
   I traced `r11 = LERP(0xC6590)` (dir1) and `r7 = LERP(0xC65A4)` (dir2): their outputs feed **ONLY** the
   max chain that becomes the float twins `lp`/`r20` (r11→r9→lp/r20; r7→r2→lp), are NOT reused as a sign,
   and are NOT stored elsewhere. So doubling their Y values cleanly doubles the float twin's corridor arm.

3. **`0xC6664` is the ENVELOPE (LERP_B), not the corridor** (confirms V26's rest-fault). It feeds the early
   envelope `lp → gp-0x6da8` and the separate envelope monitor `gp-0x6c84` (decomp line ~1287), which is
   nonzero at rest. **Left STOCK in V29.**

## Why V29 holds where V25/V26/V27 faulted

| Build | edit | failure | mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| V25 | int corridor `0x774e` ×2 only | hard fault at full lock | int wall corridor arm 2×, float twin 1× → desync (corridor-dominant at lock) |
| V26 | V25 + `0xC6664` ×2 | hard fault AT REST | `0xC6664` is the ENVELOPE; +offset at rest → envelope monitor desync |
| V27 | V25 + trampoline doubling the WHOLE float twin | hard fault when turning | doubled the IIR/boost arms too → demand-dominated twin 2× vs wall 1× |
| **V29** | int `0x774e` ×2 **+ float mirror `0xC6598`/`0xC65AC` ×2** | — | doubles **ONLY the matched corridor arm on both sides**; IIR & boost arms untouched → max stays consistent; **stock ±5/1024 residual NOT amplified** |

The structural key: V27's flaw was that doubling the entire float twin **amplified the stock float-vs-int
residual** (the secondary max arm). V29 doubles only the corridor arm — a single matched flat table on each
side — so every other arm (and its residual) is left exactly stock. The monitors stay fully live: a
genuinely-wrong corridor (~1024 LSB) still diverges far outside ±5 → recalibration, not blinding.
