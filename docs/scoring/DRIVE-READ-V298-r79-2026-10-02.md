## 1. Attribution, and the LIVE / D-sign verdict

**What ran (EVIDENCE):**
- EPS carFw `39990-TVA,A16A` on bus 1, image `_v298_…C3REV2P…A16A`, fork Dom `2712e1336` in angle mode (angleState on 100 % of rows).
- `AccordEpsAngleLoop` = 1. The learned steer ratio was pinned at 16.84.
- Fork byte-2 arm = 2 on 120 763 of 120 763 frames.
- STEER_STATUS = 0 and 0x14A b4 = 7 on all 67 073 engaged frames. 0 counter skips.
- A2 was clean on 8 of 8 request drops (tap 0 by +150 ms).

**The loop is LIVE and the right way round:**
- c_meas/c_raw = **−1.00** [−1.01, −1.00] from M1's windowed replay. The instrument's own literal regression gives −1.08.
- P is delivered at 0.93–0.96 × the image arithmetic. The speed schedule is live: dip 0.456, hi 1.504.

**D opposes motion in every band.** Seven estimators agree, from three analysts' independent code:
- M1 frequency-response ratio: τ > 0 in 200 of 200 bootstraps.
- M1 2–5 Hz regression: c_D +0.43 to +0.57.
- M1 windowed replay: d +0.79 to +0.95 × design.
- M3, once the regressor offsets are removed: c_D +0.63 / +0.64.
- Judge, 2–5 Hz with two independent ω sources: **+0.30 to +0.49, positive at every lag from 0 to 80 ms**.
- Judge, 0.3–3 Hz: +0.21 to +0.48.
- The instrument's own literal regression: +0.566.

The design value is 0.566 tap/(deg/s), with a LIVE band of [0.45, 0.70].

**The pre-registered R1 "INVERTED" stop band is WITHDRAWN.**
- It fired on one estimator only: the structural regression, c_D −0.135.
- That regression uses the instrument's replayed integrator as a regressor. The replayed I carries step offsets from two replay errors (EVIDENCE, M3):
  - it ran the **direction-0 engage ramp (+33/−16)**, but the arm-2 frames select **direction 2 (328/66)**, per image cells 0xC63FA/FC;
  - it placed the torque word on the 0x18F frame, while the firmware's word **leads 0x18F by 10 ticks** (replay R² peaks at +10 ticks: 0.928 vs 0.834).
- Errors-in-variables then drags the I coefficient to 0.41, which is where c_I/c_P 1.24 comes from, and drags d to −0.24.
- M3 reproduces −0.24 with the instrument's regressor and recovers d +1.12 with the offsets removed. That is EVIDENCE by reproduction.
- The other R1 clause, c_meas/c_raw > 0, never fired: −0.69 structural, −1.00 to −1.08 elsewhere.

**Ki is live at design strength:** c_I/c_P = 2.81 /s by M3's two methods and 2.5–2.9 /s by the judge's 0.3–3 Hz cumsum regressor, against a design value of 2.79. Freezes reduce its *effective* strength (§2, note 2).

**The R3\* fire at t = 1089.4 s is not a ring.** The judge checked it:
- θ was monotone non-increasing on 100 % of steps from 6.2° to 0.3° over 1.6 s.
- The rate was above +0.25 deg/s on only 2.5–4.4 % of frames.
- It is a pulsed stick-slip unwind under a moving setpoint, an instance of note 4(B). The ring stop band's intent is not met.

**No other stop band fired:** R2, R4, R5, R6, R7 and R8 are all clean. **R9, the operator's words, is the operator's call.**

**The camera:**
- It commanded on 3906 frames in 2 episodes, both with arm 0, during fork engagement. **The flight prerequisite "camera LKAS off" was violated.**
- The panda **forwarded** all 3906 frames to bus 0 (3906 request-1 echoes on src 128). It did not block them; the instrument's wording "blocked by the panda" is wrong.
- **No 0xE4 from any other node appeared on bus 1 after 38.39 s.** The EPS never saw the camera (EVIDENCE: M1, and the judge's counts).
- The arm gate (0 ≠ 2) was therefore not exercised.

## 4. The "6×" question

**What "6×" refers to:**
- It is the lane-torque ceiling relative to **stock**: 2461 vs 399 T, every authority cell ×6.000. That was re-verified from the images in `docs/STATE.md`: EVIDENCE of record, inherited.
- The cells are **byte-identical in V282, V294 and V298**: SCL 15360, fwd 5346, oa 992, ob 507 (M2, read from the three images; EVIDENCE).
- The goal itself binds *"authority stays inside the existing clamps"*. No build since V282 could raise it.

**Answer, with the numbers:**
- V298 delivered at most **1464 T = 3.7× the stock rail and 0.59× its own**. Its hard-frame p99 of 1080 T is 2.7× the stock rail.
- That is **0.75–0.92× V282 and V294's hard-frame p99**.
- Its wheel rate in hard manoeuvres matches V282's (p99 140 vs 142–144 deg/s) and is **half of V294's (276)**.

**The link that capped it on this route:**
- **Hands-off: the fork's 120 deg/s setpoint rate cap.** It binds on 50–52 % of hard frames. The wheel follows the capped setpoint at reach 0.998.
- **Hands-on or twist: O1.** The setpoint follows the wheel, the fade is ×0.30, and the I is frozen.
- The firmware rail was never approached.
- The error clip bound on 0.10 % of frames.

**Ruled out:** the firmware's ceiling and the firmware's gain (p 0.93–0.96 × the image).

**BELIEF** (the `values.py` sizing comment, a model): the servo's rail-limited rate is 467–483 deg/s at ≤ 10 m/s. V294 reached a rate peak of 372 on the wire.

## 5. The constraints every design candidate must now satisfy (measurements, not designs)

**C1. The ceiling is not the binder.**
- Tap ≤ 183 LSB (59–60 % of the rail) and 0 frames ≥ 300 (EVIDENCE).
- A candidate that claims more authority must show which link it moves: setpoint rate, O1, freeze, A3/ICL or stiffness. Its tap must be predicted against today's 183 / p99 135.

**C2. The 120 deg/s cap binds on 50–52 % of hands-off hard frames** (61–67 % below 8 m/s).
- Demand p99 is 321 deg/s.
- Desired leads the applied setpoint by > 30° on 23 % of those frames (EVIDENCE).

**C3. Lifting the rate cap exposes the error clip next.**
- The pre-limiter demand exceeds `ANGLE_ERROR_MAX(v)` on **49–55 % of hands-off hard frames below 8 m/s**, and on 31.5–31.9 % of all latActive frames there. These are judge numbers; M3's "31 %" is this same quantity.
- The clip caps P at **35–40 % of the rail below 8 m/s** and **16–18 % at ≥ 11.75 m/s** (M2, from image cells and `values.py`; EVIDENCE).
- The clip is **untested at highway**: 6 s of hard frames above 12.5 m/s.

**C4. The integral's ceiling is the A3 bound, not the ICL, in low-speed turns.**
- BELIEF: the design §1.4 arithmetic. It is supported by M3's replay: R² 0.928 with A3, 0.247 without.
- The bound is (16·\|θ_counts\| + 1250) S at ≤ 12.5 m/s, **capped at 4096 S ≈ 82 tap LSB ≈ 27 % of the rail at ≤ 6 m/s**.
- At θ ≈ 0 it is 1250 S ≈ 25 LSB (8 %). The ICL is 8192 S ≈ 164 LSB (53 %).
- Using M1's measured 0.02003 tap LSB per S, steady P + I below 6 m/s is ≤ about 62–65 % of the rail. The observed peak was 59 %.
- In manoeuvres the I sat at the A3 bound on 7.6 % of frames and never at the ICL (M3).

**C5. O1 trips on the hands-off reaction twist.**
- The hands-off torque word p90 is **607–681 below 8 m/s**, above both the firmware's 512 freeze and O1's 614 gp (600 raw).
- 345 of 415 O1 episodes never set steeringPressed (threshold 1200).
- O1 covers 41.5 % / 36.4 % of latActive time at < 5 / 5–8 m/s.
- The reaction fit is gp-0x4f60 ≈ −0.69·α − 0.69·ω − 163·sgn ω − 61 (R² 0.31, M3; EVIDENCE for the sign, BELIEF for the model).
- **Any threshold must be sized against this twist, and the hand-vs-twist split is still unresolved.**

**C6. The freeze duty.**
- Hard 5.1 % and opposing 6.3 % of settled hands-off time.
- 31 % of commanded integration is discarded (42–48 % below 8 m/s).
