### 5.3 WHY — and it is not the cave's fault

The loop contains **five** floor-toward-−∞ shifts. The cave repairs **one**. Removing the others one at
a time, byte-exact, fit 225, `sp = +3` (all ratios against LINEAR V282 = 0.279695):

| quantisers made exact | V292 | V282 |
|---|---|---|
| none (as built) | 0.26804 → **0.9583** | 0.26804 → 0.9583 |
| + P (`>>8`) | 0.9583 | 0.9583 |
| + D (`>>3`) | 0.9583 | 0.9583 |
| + fade (`>>8`) | 0.9583 | 0.9583 |
| **+ output lag (`>>10`,`>>10`,`>>5`)** | **0.9919** | 1.1979 |
| + motor gain (`>>15`) | 1.0945 | 1.1335 |
| lag + gain | 1.0410 | 1.1999 |
| everything but the fb filter | **1.0350** | 1.2649 |

**Read the fifth row.** With the output-lag floors removed, V292 lands at ×0.9919 — inside the clause —
while V282 goes to ×1.1979. That is the signature of a build whose own stage is correct and whose
neighbours are not. At `sp = 3` the delivered torque is ≈ 4 counts and `y` ≈ 25; a half-LSB loss in
`(t+t')>>5` and in `(y·5346)>>15` is a percent-scale error on a 4-count quantity.

⇒ **Mean-exactness of the feedback filter does not make the closed loop mean-exact.** The design's
proof (i) is true and the closed-loop clause still fails. A V293 that wanted to close this clause would
have to carry error feedback through the **output lag** as well, which is a second cave on the engaged
path — a materially bigger build than V292.

### 5.4 Severity — stated plainly, because the rule and the physics disagree

- **This is not a safety objection.** At `sp = 3` the absolute spread is 0.22 → 0.33 deg/s. There is no
  instability anywhere (§3), authority at real demand is unchanged (`sp` = 330: 0/21 outside ±1 %), and
  the direction at `+3` is *less* assist, not more.
- **V292 is a large improvement on the failing clause.** Worst |error| at `sp` = 3: **V291 55 %,
  V292 22 %**; median |error| **37 % → 6.4 %**. The **sign asymmetry** at ±3 counts — the thing that
  would read as "one-sided pull at rest" — goes **V282 ×1.33, V291 ×1.41, V292 ×0.89**: V292 is the
  smallest of the three and is the only one that is close to symmetric.
- **But the clause is ±1 % and V292 misses it on 20–21 of 21 fits at ±3 counts**, and 91 % of engaged
  time sits at small demand. **DO-NOT-FLASH is what the pre-registration says; whether that is the right
  call is the orchestrator's, and I would not defend a `do-not-flash` on physics here.**

### 7.1 🛑 A UNIT DEFECT IN THE INHERITED r24 FOLD — ×6.13

**EVIDENCE.** `b_of_f_v282.py` defines `R_r24(f) = −(gain/1024)·D4(f)·B(f)` and documents it as *"the
CELL `gp−0x6ada` per unit of x"*. `r24_lane.py` and `r24_lane_transfer.py` then form
`L_r24 = R_r24·CPD·G` and `L_tot = (R_servo + R_r24)·CPD·G`, and `ADV-V291-B` §4.2 folds with
`Λ = −CPD·R_r24`. **But `R_servo` is `T` per raw count of `x` and carries `K = 5346/32768 = 0.163147`
(`Blocks.R()` multiplies by `self.K`), while `R_r24` is a CELL value and does not.** The r24 cell sums
into the same aggregate `r11` that the LKAS term is added to at `0x2A1FC`, and that aggregate is scaled
by `(r11·(−5346))>>15` at `0x2A1FE`. **Proof it is the same aggregate:** on the disengaged path
`0x2A164`–`0x2A172` the LKAS term is forced to zero and `r11` is the *sole* source of delivered torque,
still scaled by `×(−5346)>>15`. ⇒ **the r24 lane's loop transmission is `Λ = K·CPD·R_r24`, not
`CPD·R_r24`, and the fold as documented over-weights the r24 lane by ×6.13.**

**Direction of the error: it makes the folded numbers look WORSE than they are.** Recomputing with the
motor gain included:

| quantity | record `Λ` | `Λ × 5346/32768` | unfolded |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Ms` 12–26 reduction, k 0.895 | ×3.824 | **×5.194** | ×5.345 |
| folded `f0` median | 17.76 Hz | **18.42 Hz** | — |
| folded vector margin (V292) | 0.1974 | **0.2673** | 0.2750 |
| folded ring ratio | ×3.466 | **×3.922** | ×3.258 |

**Nothing in this pass turns on it** — B3 and B6 pass either way, and B2's gate uses the *measured* arms
`LS73`/`LR73`, not `R_r24`, so it is untouched. **BELIEF, one step:** I have not read `advB`'s fold code
(its scratch files are gone), so I cannot prove its implementation matched its documentation. **EVIDENCE:**
the documented formula, and the `×5346>>15` on the shared aggregate. **If the record's folded numbers were
computed as documented, `ADV-V291-B` §4.2's "flown-arm FAIL" (×1.44–1.82) and §11's margins are both
pessimistic**, and the `f0` numbers that were compared against the 15–17 Hz band V289 was rejected in are
0.7 Hz low.

### 7.2 🛑 THE ±102 DEADBAND ON `y` CAN MAKE THE ENTIRE `sp = 3` CLAUSE VACUOUS

**EVIDENCE, `0x2A1AE`–`0x2A1E4`.** With `[0xC64A3] = 1` (**it is 1 in this image**) the code falls into a
gate on `gp−0x6806`; when that byte is **0**, `y` is zeroed unless `|y| > [0xC61B8] = 102` or
`sign(y) == sign(gp−0x6b30)`. **At `sp` = 3 the steady `y` is ≈ 25 counts — deep inside the deadband.**
`gp−0x6806` is written by an engage state machine (`0x293A6` sets it to 1 alongside `gp−0x679f := 1`,
`gp−0x3d38 := 3`; `0x29696` sets it to 0 alongside `gp−0x679f := 6`, `gp−0x3d38 := 8`). **I did not
resolve which state is live during engaged LKAS.**

Byte-exact, with the deadband modelled:

| fit | `sp` | V282 off → on | V292 off → on |
|---|---|---|---|
| median | 3 | 0.26804 → **0.00000** | 0.26804 → **0.00000** |
| median | 33 | 3.10214 → 3.10214 | 3.05600 → 3.05634 |
| median | 330 | 30.81306 → 30.81306 | 30.69103 → 30.69103 |
| hi-auth | 33 | 3.98711 → 3.87877 | 3.86104 → 3.75031 |

⇒ **If `gp−0x6806` is 0 when engaged, then at `sp = 3` both builds deliver exactly zero and the B4
clause — V291's FAIL and V292's entire reason for existing — is 0/0.** This is the largest open premise
under this whole line of work and it is not in either pre-registration. **BELIEF on which branch is
live; EVIDENCE on the code and on the numbers above.** It belongs to surface D to close.

   is spent on the sp = 3 point, on either side.**
