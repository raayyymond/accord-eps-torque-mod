`accord-the-override-taper-arm-is-selected-by-a-0xe4-field-openpilot-sends-0…` established is **never

## F5 — **FIRES**: the ring-margin formula scales the wrong thing, and the gate is detector-fragile

**(a) `Ls + Lr == 1` is a convention, not a measurement** — `stutter_v283_memoryless.py` writes
`Lr = 1.0 - Ls` explicitly, overwriting the measured `Lr`. That is dimensionally fine (a decomposition of
unity) and is **not** the defect. `Ls = zT/(zT+zr)` is genuinely measured.

**(b) The defect is `Ls*(Kp/248)`.** That assumes the servo arm's gain is proportional to Kp. From the
V284 image it is not: the summing node is **P + D**, with `P = E*Kp>>8` and `D = dE*128>>3` — and **Kd is a
flat-128 LERP (`0xE511C`), so D does not scale with Kp at all.** At f0 = 7 Hz on the 1 kHz grid D is in
quadrature with P and |D|/|P| = **0.73** at Kp 248. The true servo scaling is
`(Kp/256 + jD)/(248/256 + jD)`:

```
Kp 341:  true 1.258 at  -8.1 deg   vs model 1.375   (magnitude overstated x1.09)
Kp 512:  true 1.771 at -16.6 deg   vs model 2.065   (magnitude overstated x1.17)
```

The magnitude error is in the safe direction. **The phase error is not, and it dominates:** rotating the
servo arm -16.6 deg swings it toward `Lr`'s large positive real part, so the vector sum *grows*. Gate
recomputed at the plateau Kp 512, `|L_tot(248)| = 0.90`:

```
        model x0.90        true (P+jD) x0.90
r35        0.933                1.142   RE-ARMS
r36        1.162                1.314   RE-ARMS
r37        0.941                1.114   RE-ARMS
r38        0.953                1.104   RE-ARMS
```

**(c) Independently, the gate is not robust to the ring detector.** Under my own ring detector (121.3 s of
ring vs the study's 8.9 s), the worst-episode gate under **the build's own formula** is **1.076**, not the
reported 0.949 — it re-arms. Ranking the candidates the build compared, worst episode over all four routes:

```
                    build's formula, my detector   |   corrected (P+jD) scaling
V282 flat 248              0.900                   |        0.900
M1  flat 341               0.949                   |        1.033  RE-ARMS
M2  flat 400               1.007  RE-ARMS          |        1.126  RE-ARMS
M8* (V284)                 1.076  RE-ARMS          |        1.216  RE-ARMS
```

**M8\* is not the safest of the candidates on either correction — it is the least safe.** The build's
selection of M8\* over M1/M2 rested on the 0.949-vs-1.007 split, and that split does not survive either a
different ring detector or the corrected servo scaling.

**(d) Structural: there is no headroom to gate on.** At Kp 248 the gate reads exactly 0.900 *by
construction* (ratio == 1), so the entire margin is `1/|L_tot(248)| = 1.11x` and `|L_tot(248)| = 0.90` is a
**BELIEF**. Under the corrected scaling, M8\* becomes marginal at `|L_tot(248)| = 0.740` — far below the
prereg's 0.88–0.91. On the corrected formula, **no value in the prereg range leaves V284 with margin.**

`np.array_equal` sanity check against `SV.sim_ki` on flat 248 before reporting anything. **Any X-axis edit
needs a chain that takes `kpX` explicitly.**
