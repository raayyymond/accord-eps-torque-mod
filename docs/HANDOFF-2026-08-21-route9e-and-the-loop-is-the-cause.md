## 3. ⭐⭐ THE BIG RESULT — THE LOOP IS IDENTIFIED

From the 4×/8× gain steps (routes `0x85` = V100, `0x95` = V101), both of which packed the aggregator
**SUM** on the 427 lane:

```
|kG| = 0.630  [0.512, 1.001]        gain margin ~1.2-1.6
A = 1+P = 0.440 @ +25.0 deg          closed-loop amplification 1/|A| = 2.28x  [1.51, 9.4x]
Z0 = 2792 @ -92.45 deg               Re(Z0)/|Z0| = -0.043   <- NEAR-LOSSLESS SPRING
```

⇒ **100 % of the measured `Re(Z) = −3761` at 6–9 Hz is LOOP-GENERATED.** The 8 Hz mode is the assist
loop's own near-instability, not a passive resonance being excited.

**Three checks the solve was free to fail:** it recovered `κ`'s sign independently (`arg c = +145.3°`
vs a structural +180°, the gap being **exactly 12.8 ms of actuation lag at 7.5 Hz**); the identified
plant came back physical; and it **replicated within a single drive** off the torque ladder.

### 3.1 🛑 THE LAW THIS PRODUCED

> **At 6–9 Hz the aggregator is a 4:1 near-CANCELLATION — individual lanes are LARGER than their sum
> (`coh²(T, sum)` = 0.279 vs `coh²(T, lane)` = 0.80–0.89). ANY single-lane perturbation is amplified
> ~4× at the output. SIZE EVERY 6–9 Hz LEVER AGAINST THE SUM (0.053), NEVER AGAINST A LANE.**

Band-specific: at 21.0–22.5 Hz the ratio is only 1.68. **This retroactively explains why sixty builds
of lane-sized doses produced nothing, or the opposite of what was predicted.**

### 3.2 Sizing, quantitative for the first time

`ΔP = c·ΔG`, `|c| = 13.09`. **`ΔG = 0.047`** (89 % of the whole sum) takes **`|A|` 0.44 → 0.87**.
A lane-sized dose is **~4× over**. A **wrong sign** at that size drives `|A|` to **0.15 — a 6.7×
amplification.**

### 7.1 🛑🛑 THE `ld.bu` DISPLACEMENT TRAP — invalidates prior byte-cell scans
**The displacement's LOW BIT lives in `hw1` bit 5, not `hw2`.** Ghidra: `85 67 fb 74` →
`ld.bu 0x74fa,tp,r12` (`0xC64FA`) but `a5 7f fb 74` → `ld.bu 0x74fb,tp,r15` (`0xC64FB`) — **identical
`hw2`**. The kit's rule (*"`ld.bu` → `hw2 == enc|1`"*) is **WRONG** and has been **conflating ADJACENT
CELLS** — the first trap that merges two *different* cells rather than miscounting one.
**Confirmed casualty:** `0xC64FA` has **8** readers, not the recorded 18 "cross-validated two ways";
the other **10 read `0xC64FB`**, an uncharacterised cal. Both validations shared the blind spot.
**Adjudicate every byte-load with `disassemble_bytes`, never with displacement bytes.**
