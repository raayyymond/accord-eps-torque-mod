1 · Where the edits sit in the LKAS demand path

openpilot's 0xE4 command becomes a demand index, the index is looked up in the assist map to give a rate reference, the rate loop pushes until the measured wheel rate matches it, and the push is scaled and clamped into motor torque. V37 doubled the push (gain and clamps). V37A rev 3 keeps that and makes the ask a straight line to twice Honda's top (the map, drawn in the node), with the feedback clamp doubled so the loop can still see the wheel at the new rates.

5 · Risk before the drive, and what one short drive will tell

Authority stays 2×; the reference doubles at the top and drops at small signal. Peak LKAS torque is 820 counts, 2× Honda's, as on V37; V37 reached it only at full demand, rev 3 reaches it from idx 128 at a stalled wheel. The reference at full demand is 344 counts (≈44.5 deg/s) against stock's 172 (≈22). Because Honda's map is concave and the new one is a line, the reference is below stock until idx ≈ 77 (17 vs 24 at idx 12, 0.71×): small lane-keeping corrections ask for a slower wheel than stock while being pushed 2× as hard toward it. Above idx 77 the ask grows toward 2×.

P rails from idx 128 at a stalled wheel; stock never rails. V280 (same linear reference shape, ×6 torque) flew r32/r33 with the operator reporting "a small signal riding on top of a large one" in strong turns, the 7 Hz P-desaturation mechanism. V37A rev 3 reaches that mechanism from idx 128 at 1/3 of V280's torque amplitude (820 vs 2461) and with a 44.5 deg/s reference instead of V280's 134. BELIEF: milder than r32/r33; not flown.

Low-demand damping. In the region where V276 rang (idx ≤ 58) the line sits at 0.7–1.1× Honda's reference, below the K = 2 regime V278 rev 3 flew clean, so the lane opposes the wheel at least as often as stock does there. BELIEF for this image.

Trips that stay off (as on every build since V36/V37): the STEER_STATUS-4 debounce and the DTC 0x49 over-torque counter. Honda's EME corridor is ×4 looser (V25–V31, carried).

Pre-registered read (free on the wire, one engaged episode). The clean signal is full-demand, hands-light steering rate: stock/V112 gave ≈24 deg/s and V278 (the same 2× top with Honda's shape, at 6× torque) 42 deg/s; rev 3 asks 44.5 at 2× torque, expect near V278's. At small demand (idx ≤ 75) the rate per idx should read slightly below stock (0.7–1×), not above. The 0xE4 RX-fault sentinel pulse (openpilot drops the message) is 820 counts for 2 s chasing 44.5 deg/s; on V37 it was 794 counts chasing 22. If felt, cal 0xC63F6 16→328 shortens it to 0.1 s.

The sentence a null licenses. If the full-demand rate is unchanged from V37 (≈24 deg/s), the map is not the live setpoint source on this image (selector ≠ 7) and the build is inert, not harmful. There is no outcome that cannot be read.

Four independent agents on the rev-1 image, each re-deriving from the image and each able to return "do not flash", then focused re-checks of the rev-2 delta (torque cells kept) and the rev-3 delta (the straight line to 344, clamp 15360).
