1 · Where the edits sit in the LKAS demand path

openpilot's 0xE4 command becomes a demand index, the index is looked up in the assist map to give a rate reference, the rate loop pushes until the measured wheel rate matches it, and the push is scaled and clamped into motor torque. V37 doubled the push (gain and clamps). V37A rev 2 keeps that and also doubles the ask (the map, drawn in the node), with the feedback clamp scaled so the loop can still see the wheel at the new rates.

5 · Risk before the drive, and what one short drive will tell

Authority stays 2×, and arrives earlier; the reference rises. Peak LKAS torque is 820 counts, 2× Honda's, as on V37, but V37 reached it only at full demand while rev 2 reaches it from idx 63 (26 % of the demand axis) at a stalled wheel. The reference at full demand is 960 counts (≈124 deg/s) against stock's 172 (≈22 deg/s): 5.6× at the top, 2× at small signal. Small corrections move the wheel faster and harder than on V37.

P rails from idx 63 at a stalled wheel; stock never rails. V280 (same linear reference shape, ×6 torque) flew r32/r33 with the operator reporting "a small signal riding on top of a large one" in strong turns, the 7 Hz P-desaturation mechanism. V37A rev 2 reaches that mechanism at 1/3 of V280's torque amplitude (820 vs 2461). BELIEF: milder than r32/r33; not flown.

Low-demand damping. At idx ≤ 12 the lane is exactly the K = 2 reference V278 rev 3 flew clean (no 3.9 Hz ring); across idx 32–58 the line sits 2.0–2.6× stock, between V278 (0.863 damping fraction) and V280's 4.3/idx line (0.840 simulated). BELIEF for this image.

Trips that stay off (as on every build since V36/V37): the STEER_STATUS-4 debounce and the DTC 0x49 over-torque counter. Honda's EME corridor is ×4 looser (V25–V31, carried).

Pre-registered read (free on the wire, one engaged episode). Small/medium demand (idx ≤ 60, 91 % of engaged time): achieved wheel rate per idx doubles against V37 or stock (0.26 → 0.52 deg/s per idx). Sustained full demand, hands light: stock/V112 gave ≈24 deg/s, V278 (2× map at 6× torque) 42 deg/s; rev 2 asks 124 at 2× torque, expect between those. The 0xE4 RX-fault sentinel pulse (openpilot drops the message) is 820 counts for 2 s chasing 123 deg/s; on V37 it was 794 counts chasing 22. If felt, cal 0xC63F6 16→328 shortens it to 0.1 s.

The sentence a null licenses. If rate per idx on straight-road corrections is unchanged from V37, the map is not the live setpoint source on this image (selector ≠ 7) and the build is inert, not harmful. There is no outcome that cannot be read.

Four independent agents on the rev-1 image (identical to rev 2 except the three torque cells), each re-deriving from the image and each able to return "do not flash", then a focused re-check of the rev-2 delta. Fail conditions were written before they ran: an overflow or signed read of the new values; a wrong unit factor ≥ 1.5×; an unexplained byte, stale CRC or non-reproducible hash; a downstream threshold the new setpoint, error or railed P crosses that stock did not.
