3. ✅ **I independently re-verified, from the V282 image opened in Ghidra this session**, that `T =
   gp-0x6b38` is still the CAN427 payload source (`0x55DF0 ld.h -0x6b38,gp,r6`, then `andi 0xffff`, shift-
   and-mask into `(sign<<9)|(|T|>>3)`, mask `0x3FF` at `0x55E04`) and that the feedback EMA's two cal reads
   (`ld.hu 0x73ea,tp,r16` / `ld.h 0x73e8,tp,r9` at `0x28F86`/`0x28F8A`, `add r9,r26` at `0x28FA4`) match
   923/1560 byte-for-byte against a fresh Python LE read of the same file. [EVIDENCE, this session]
   `gp-0x6a56`'s identity as the 0x18F wire signal and as this EMA's upstream input is corroborated by
   three independent prior-session memories with byte citations
   (`accord-gp6a56-is-motor-rate-not-an-angle-sensor.md`,
   `accord-feedback-operand-is-a-two-sample-sum-dc-30-89.md`,

   own `ld` instruction this session, so that specific link is BELIEF-leaning-EVIDENCE, not re-derived by
   me. It is not load-bearing for §0's verdict either way — even a different exact register carrying the
   same physical quantity would not change the recommendation.
4. 🛑 **The honest answer on ring excitation (the brief's most important question), computed fresh for
   this build [EVIDENCE, self-derived from the pidframe/loopshape arithmetic, this session]:**
   at `Kp=0, Kd=128, Ki=0` the **servo lane's own damping goes UP at both symptom frequencies**, not down:
   - **7.3 Hz ring margin**: `|L(7.3)|` (pooled `Ls`/`Lr` split, loopshape §4) falls from **0.976 → 0.842**
     at the point estimate (0.990 → 0.855 pessimistic) — a *larger* improvement than any single (pole, Kd)
     candidate in loopshape's table except the largest pole raise.
   - **20 Hz damping budget**: `Re_total` (servo + `s·r24`) rises from **+2.06 → +2.59** (+25%), because
     losing the phase-flat `P` term rotates the servo phase from −65° toward −44° at 20 Hz — closer to
     the axis that reads as damping in this convention, not further from it.
   - **⇒ V285 is predicted to be QUIETER at both symptom frequencies than V282/V283, not louder.** This
     is the opposite of a naive "less loop gain → less everything" read, and it is the single most
     important thing this design study found. It means: (a) a sustained self-oscillation is **not**
     expected on V285 itself — consistent with the operator's own framing that Kd=128 is a *starting
     point* to raise from, not already at `Ku`; (b) the loop's overall low-frequency authority still
     collapses (`|C·Hlag|` at 1 Hz falls to 10% of the Kp=248 baseline, confirmed by direct computation),

     high-frequency (ring-band) response — the D term's own regime; (c) **the ring will most likely show
     up as a well-damped TRANSIENT after a disturbance (a bump, a quick correction, a stall release),
     not as a standing hum** — which is exactly the shape a decay-envelope tap needs, and exactly why
     candidate 1's *purpose* (measure Q, extrapolate `Ku`) is right even though the specific tap is
     unnecessary.
5. ⚠ **Caveat on the excitation prediction, stated plainly**: this is a *servo-arm-only* re-derivation of

## 2. The four candidates, ranked and verdicted as asked

### Candidate 1 — limit-cycle decay tap on `gp-0x3d3c`

- **(a) readout field / occupancy**: none exists yet; would need a new CAN field or cave magnitude channel.
  `gp-0x3d3c` currently has **zero** wire presence.
- **(b) byte cost**: not costed — see verdict.
- **(c) GATE 1/2**: GATE 1 closed by loopshape (private-in-effect, four accesses image-wide, orphan
  unreachable by five arguments — one an admitted `jmp[reg]` gap). GATE 2: N/A, read-only.
- **(d) null-licensing sentence**: *"`gp-0x3d3c`'s envelope decay at 18-22 Hz gives a Q that, extrapolated,
  bounds `Ku`."*
- **(e) verdict: DO NOT BUILD for V285.** `gp-0x3d3c` sits *inside* the controller — it is upstream of the
  output gain, the engagement ramp, and (per the operator's own standing rule) it is **more** "the lever's
  own output" than `T` already is, not less. Everything this tap would buy for a **decay/Q** read, `T`
  (already flown, zero cost) already buys, and everything it would buy for **GATE 1 confidence on a future
  Kd raise**, loopshape's document already bought by argument, with its own recommended read-only tap
  costed and ready **if a future Kd-raising build wants it**. Building it now spends cave-bit budget and a
  new interlock/CRC review for a channel `V285` does not need. **Recommendation: defer this exact tap,
  unchanged, to whichever future build in the sweep first raises Kd — it is loopshape's probe, not a new
  one, and nothing about V285 changes its cost-benefit.**

### Candidate 2 — damping sign bit, `sign(D) != sign(fb)`

- **(a)**: none exists as a new onboard rung. **But the equivalent measurement is already flying and
  already decoded**: `sign(T) != sign(0x18F rate)` was the flown methodology for V278 rev 3
  (`accord-v278r3-torque-tap-reads-310-and-damping-is-sign-t-ne-sign-rate.md`) and needs no firmware
  change to reuse on V285 — it is computed **offline** from two CAN messages already on the wire.
- **(c) GATE 1/2**: N/A — no new code.
- **(d)**: *"`P(sign(T) != sign(0x18F rate))` in engaged, disturbed (post-transient) frames is > 0.5 and
  moves with the predicted `Re` sign."*
- **(e) verdict: DO NOT BUILD.** An onboard version would only buy tighter time-alignment between the two
  operands (no cross-message interpolation), which matters for a **phase** measurement, not for the
  **sign-duty** statistic V278 rev 3 already validated. §3 flags one real difference (D and T share sign
  essentially always under Kp=0/Ki=0, since neither the sum clamp nor the taper is expected to bite — see
  below) which makes an onboard D-vs-fb tap and the offline T-vs-rate read **the same measurement** for
  this specific build, not two.

### Candidate 3 — `|D| >= |P|` comparator

- **(e) verdict: DO NOT BUILD — CONFIRMED TAUTOLOGICAL**, as the brief already suspected. Byte-verified
  this session: V282's (and hence V285's, since V285 only edits the Kp record) `P` term is `(E·Kp)>>8`
  with `Kp` forced to the flat-zero LERP: `|P| ≡ 0` on every tick by construction, so `|D| ≥ |P|` reads
  **1.000 on every frame with `D != 0`, and `0/0` (implementation-defined, likely reads as `>=` true too)
  when `D` is also zero.** There is no replacement two-term comparator to build in its place, because with
  `Ki=0` too, `S = D` exactly (up to the 254/256 taper, which is 1.000 absent a driver-override cliff) —
  **there is only one term in this loop.** Any "fraction of the sum" statistic is vacuous here by
  construction, not just this one. This is a structural fact about the Kp=0/Ki=0 configuration, not a
  probe-design failure — no comparator rung can extract a ratio from a controller with one live term.

### Candidate 4 — magnitude channel for `|D|` or `|S|`, sized against its own clamp

- **(a)**: `T` (`gp-0x6b38`) already **is** this channel in substance: `S ≈ D` exactly under V285's
  configuration (see Candidate 3), and `T` is `S` after the output lag, the engagement ramp, and the
  forward gain — the actually-delivered version of the same quantity, already sized against its own reach
  (`|T|>>3`, ceiling `0xC61B4`=3072, occupying CAN 0x1AB/427 bits 0-8, sign at bit 9, `((b0&3)<<8)|b1`).
- **(c)**: N/A, no new code.
- **(d)**: *"`|T|` p50 in engaged disturbed frames, and the fraction of frames at `|field| >= 309`
  (saturation)."*
- **(e) verdict: DO NOT BUILD a new channel.** `T` already satisfies GATE 3 (sized against its own lane's
  reachable output, not a downstream clamp — the `0xC61B4` clamp IS the lane's own ceiling here, since `S`
  never reaches the separate sum clamp `0xC61BE` under this configuration: `|D|≤10240` clamped pre-sum,
  `254/256·10240 ≈ 10160 < 15360`, so `0xC61BE` cannot bite. **One clamp for the whole loop, and `T` is
  already downstream of it.** The only genuinely new information a raw `|D|` (pre-lag) tap would add over
  `|T|` (post-lag) is the output-lag's own transfer function at 5.05 Hz — which is `gp-0x3d3c`'s job
  (Candidate 1), already deferred for the same reason.

## 3. What actually changes on the wire for V285, and how to read it

**Nothing changes.** V282's cave (`0xC4B34`, `0x14A` byte 4 bits 7/6/5/4/3 = r24 comparators, unaffected —
`FUN_0003aa2c`'s lane has no dependency on `FUN_00028ea6`'s Kp record) and the 427 torque tap (`0x55DF0`-
`0x55E11`, `T = gp-0x6b38`) carry forward unedited. `0x14A` byte4 bits 2/1/0 remain free (headroom, not
spent) if a future build in this sweep wants a new rung.

**Read it with:**
1. **Primary — `gp-0x6a56` on CAN 0x18F bytes[2:3] (100 Hz, 10× finer LSB than 0x14A's copy)**: the
   **motion**, per the operator's own scoring rule. Fit a decay envelope / per-episode ACF at the ring
   frequencies (7.3 Hz strong-turn, 18-22 Hz creep) the same way `loopshape`/`stutter283` already fit
   `|L|` — this is the direct `Ku`-extrapolation channel and it costs nothing.
2. **Secondary/cross-check — `T` on CAN 0x1AB/427 (50 Hz)**: the controller's own delivered output,
   already-decoded (`sign(T)<<9 | |T|>>3`, saturation flag `|field| >= 309`). Use it to (a) confirm the
   envelope read from `gp-0x6a56` is being driven by this loop and not some other lane, and (b) flag and
   exclude any railed frames from the decay fit — clamp headroom is generous here (`|D|` never exceeds 40%
   of its clamp under any measured V283 strong-turn frame per `pidframe` §6, and V285 has strictly less
   loop gain feeding it than V283 did at 7.3-20 Hz per §0.4, so saturation should be rarer than on record,
   not more common — worth confirming, not assuming).
3. **Unaffected but still worth reading — `0x14A` byte4 bits 6/5 (`|r24| >= |T|`, `|r24| >= aggregator`)**:
   these tell you whether the *other* lane (r24, engaged-only, untouched by this edit) still dominates the
   aggregator the way it did on V282 — if V285's drive shows those duty cycles moving, that is evidence
   the two lanes interact nonlinearly at the aggregator and the servo-arm-only model in §0.4 is
   incomplete, which is itself worth knowing.

**The sentence a null licenses, stated once for the whole build:** *"On V285, the 7.3 Hz and 18-22 Hz
envelopes measured on `gp-0x6a56` decay with time constants `τ_7` and `τ_20`; converting each to a `Q` and
comparing against the servo-arm model's predicted `Re`/`|1-L|` at Kd=128 gives a calibration point. If a
future build raises Kd and the measured `Q` moves in the direction and rough proportion the byte model
predicts, `Ku` can be bracketed from two points instead of swept blind. If V285 shows no measurable ring
at all in ~20-30 s of engaged, disturbed driving, that is not an instrument failure — §0.4 predicts exactly
that as the LIKELY outcome, and the correct response is to seek a rougher/more disturbed engaged segment
for the read, not to conclude the tap is broken or to add new telemetry reflexively."**

## 4. What I did NOT include, and why

- **A new onboard comparator or magnitude rung of any kind.** Every candidate in the brief reduces to a
  channel that is either already flying (T, 0x18F rate) or structurally vacuous under Kp=0/Ki=0 (any
  P-relative or sum-fraction statistic). Adding cave bits here would violate the session's own
  "prefer the inert tap" law in its strongest form: the tap does not even need to be *added*, it needs to
  be *read*.
- **Any edit to the 427 packer or the cave.** Zero code bytes beyond the Kp record itself change on V285
  by this design; GATE 1/GATE 2 for telemetry are therefore moot for this build — there is no new RAM
  ownership or closed-loop concern to clear, because nothing new touches the loop.
- **A magnitude estimate for the predicted ring change.** §0.5 states why: loopshape's own §9.2 already
  showed the `ΔRe → Δamplitude` map fails by two orders of magnitude on the one real calibration point
  available. I give sign only, and say so.
- **Any assessment of the Kp=0 cal edit's own safety (GATE 1/2 on the edit itself, not the telemetry).**
  Out of scope for this brief; that is the build/adversarial agents' job, not mine.

## What would falsify this document

- **`gp-0x6a56` turning out not to be this loop's feedback-EMA input** (§0.3's one unverified link). If
  wrong, `0x18F`'s rate field is still a valid *independent* motion sensor for scoring the drive, but it
  would no longer be provably the loop's own sensed variable, and the "score the motion, it's the same
  signal the loop uses" framing in §3 would need to drop that clause (the recommendation to read it would
  not change).
- **The servo-arm-only re-derivation in §0.4** inherits every falsifier loopshape already logged for the
  same formulas (tick rate, the orphan's reachability, the operating-point interval, `Ls`/`Lr`'s validity)
  — see `LOOPSHAPE-LAGPOLE-KD-2026-09-04.md` §11 for the full list; nothing here adds a new one except that
  it is evaluated at a point (`Kp=0`) nobody had computed before.
- **A drive that shows V285 LOUDER than V282/V283 at either ring frequency.** That would not falsify the
  arithmetic in §0.4 by itself (see the caveat), but it would be the strongest evidence yet that the
  `ΔRe`/`Δ|L|` → amplitude map loopshape flagged as broken is broken in a way that matters for this sweep,
  and it should be reported as such rather than reconciled by assumption.
