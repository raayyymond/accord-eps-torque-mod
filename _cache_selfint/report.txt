S1.2  THE MODEL.  A single (J, b, k) impedance CANNOT fit this data -- the measured
      phase of Z sweeps ~180 deg across 4-30 Hz, which no memoryless J/b/k can do.
      The minimal physical model that can, given the CAN angle sits BELOW the bar:

          upper column:  J*thddot_w = T_d - T_bar - b*thdot_w,  T_bar = k*(th_w - th_p)
          hands off (T_d=0), eliminate th_w:
              Z(s) = T/thdot_p = -k (J s + b) / (J s^2 + b s + k)
                                = -k (s + 2 zeta wn) / (s^2 + 2 zeta wn s + wn^2)

      LIMITS -- this is the whole answer to the operator's question:
          2*zeta*wn << w << wn :  T -> -J*theta_ddot   INERTIAL REACTION (his mechanism)
          w >> wn              :  T -> -k*theta        STIFFNESS against a wheel that
                                                       its own inertia has pinned.
      Expressed as a coefficient on theta those two have OPPOSITE SIGN (-J*thddot =
      +J*w^2*theta), so the required cancellation INVERTS through wn.

    🛑 `react SIGN` and `model regime` are TWO INDEPENDENT READINGS of the same
    question -- the first from the sign of Imag(Z) at that frequency alone, the second
    from the wideband fn.  Where they agree the call is [EVIDENCE]; where they disagree
    the frequency is near fn and neither term dominates.

    HOW TO READ `regime`:  it is (f/fn)^2 = |J w^2| / k, i.e. which of the two terms
    of the SAME upper-column free body dominates the bar signal at that frequency.
    < 0.5  the bar IS the inertial reaction to the MEASURED angular acceleration
    > 2.0  the wheel is pinned by its own inertia; the bar is being wound up against
           it, so the bar tracks theta with the OPPOSITE sign a theta_ddot term gives.
