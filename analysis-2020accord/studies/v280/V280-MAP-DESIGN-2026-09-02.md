## 3. Kp's LERP already shapes the low end; lowering it further is a different lever

Kp(idx) = 248 at 0, 333 at idx 22 (the ringing point), 496 at 64, 696 from 136: Honda already runs the P gain at **0.48×** its top value where V276 rang.
The E-comparator is Kp-BLIND (Kp > 0), so the pre-registered metric cannot rank Kp doses. Through the chain on U2 / 2@64→6: Kp low-idx ÷2 (124,256,645,696,696)
leaves damp_E 0.863, raises damp_T ringing 0.678 → 0.753 (D's share of T grows — a phase change, not more opposing torque) and cuts |T| p50 in the ringing
frames 798 → 448. Kp flat 696 rails P on 41 % of ringing ticks. ⇒ Kp at low idx is a **complementary GAIN lever** (halves the lane's loop gain at the ringing
operating point, does not touch the ceiling, and halves small-correction authority on straights); **no Kp dose has ever flown** (V279's flat 256 is unflown),
so it has no on-car anchor. Do not stack it into V280: the map top is the lever the operator asked for, and one lever per build. [BELIEF]

## Recommendation

**V280 = rev 3 + slot-7 map 2@96→6 (Y 0 48 84 100 124 200 252 445 627 1032, all 28 records scaled the same way) + `0xC62E6` = 46080; tap unchanged.**
Predicted: ceiling crossover 133.6 deg/s (V276's); damp_E in V276's ringing frames 0.863 (= rev 3, which flew clean); tap damp_T on rev-3-like driving 0.371
(rev 3: 0.399 sim / **0.397 measured** — NOT a discriminating read, see below); saturation 0.005; D-rail 0.0067. Fallback with margin: 2@96→4 (Y240 688,
clamp 30720, ceiling 89 deg/s, sat 0.002, P-rail 0.121).

**Read it by** (the tap already on the wire): (a) sustained hands-off full-demand rate p50 — rev 3 42.3 / p90 56.4; V280 must exceed 56 or the top is not the limiter;
(b) tap |T| at idx = 240 — rev 3 p50 539; if the reference is now above the load, |T| rises toward the low-speed rail (~1240–1680). **The sentence a null licenses:**
"full-demand rate ≤ 56 deg/s with |T| p50 unchanged ⇒ the load / low-speed multiplier, not the map, limits the rate." (c) damping: pre-register per ROUTE TYPE —
the 0.60 normal-frame number was r2e's; on r31 the chain gives 0.399 and the wire read 0.397. Ringing recurrence = the r2e-style episode detector, not the fraction.

**Defects found on the way (reported, not fixed):** `decode_v278r3_torque_tap.py` decodes the field as `((d0&0x7F)<<3)|(d1>>5)`; the wire carries it as
`((d0&3)<<8)|d1` (that decoder gives |T| ≤ 176, sign never set, on r31). The pre-reg's post-sum multiplier 254/256 is falsified below 10 m/s (~0.42–0.51).
