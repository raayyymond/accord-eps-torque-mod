**Answer to question 4 [EVIDENCE, decompile + assembly]:**
- **Kp and Kd are indexed by the demand index `idx = |v|`, the SAME register the map is indexed by** — `r7` is stored to `gp-0x674b` at `0x29D14`, is only `cmp`'d until the Kp LERP walks it at `0x29DE8–0x29E12` (table base `0xCB994[sel]` loaded at `0x29DC6`), and the Kd LERP (`0xCB7D4`, `0x29E76`) takes the same value. `idx` is a function of **cmd, the driver-torque taper and speed only** (`v = ((taper·speedF)&0xFFFF)·clamp(−4·cmd,±LIMIT) >> 16 >> 6`, decompile L925–949). **Neither the map, the setpoint, E nor the feedback enters idx.**
- Therefore **doubling the map doubles dT/dcmd EXACTLY (2.00 at every idx) and changes the rate-feedback gain dT/d(rate) by EXACTLY 1.00.** The map enters only through `E = 32·sp − fb` as the reference; `dE/dfb = −1` whatever the map. The rate-feedback gain comes from **Kp(idx) (and 16·ΔE from Kd) alone**. Not "more than double": at a given cmd the Kp is the same.
- **The EPS-internal rate loop (T → plant → rate → fb → E → P/D → T) has identical small-signal gain and identical phase in V112, rev 3 and V280 at every idx and every frequency.** Its gain margin, whatever the plant is, is build-independent below the clamps.
- **What DOES change by ×2 is the OUTER path: cmd → sp → wheel rate.** For any plant G, the inner closed loop `rate/sp = 32·C·G/(1 + 8·C·G·H_fb)` is build-independent, so `rate/cmd` is exactly 2.00× V112 in rev 3 at every idx (slope ratio, Part 1) and 2.00× in level (Part 3). **That ×2 is loop gain in openpilot's loop** (cmd ← torque controller ← lateral-accel error ← angle ← ∫rate), not in the EPS PID. [EVIDENCE for the ×2; BELIEF that openpilot's loop is the one that rings — consistent with the V276 finding that the 3.9 Hz was of the combined loop, and with `sign(E)` damping-fraction results.]

**So the operator's "LKAS PID loop tuning issue" is half right:** the EPS PID's own loop is untouched; what the ×2 map did is double the gain of the loop that closes through openpilot, at every command level, with no phase change. A speed-dependent appearance (highway only) is expected there because openpilot's plant gain angle → lateral accel grows with v²; the EPS-internal loop has no speed term at all in this regime (speedF = 255 flat, LIMIT = 16384 flat above 3413 speed units, post-multiplier 254 at low driver torque).

- The P-only column reproduces the 2026-09-01 handoff's −28.5/−41/−52° at 2/3/4 Hz. **The D term (16·ΔE at 1 kHz) is NOT negligible: it is 41 % of P at 4 Hz (idx 0) and adds 15–25° of lead in 3–8 Hz**; the handoff's phase budget omitted it.
- **Firmware alone reaches −90° at 27 Hz (P+D) / 8.6 Hz (P only) and −180° at 171 Hz / 58 Hz.** With a pure-inertia plant (−90°) the EPS-internal loop would cross −180° where the firmware gives −90°, i.e. ≥ 8.6 Hz — **not 4–8 Hz unless the plant itself is resonant there**, which is what the V276 analysis concluded (3.9 Hz mechanical). The plant T → rate is **not in the kit's record** (handoff: "never characterised"), so no absolute gain margin is computable; **the ratio between builds is 1.00 at every frequency**, so the EPS-internal −180° frequency is the same on V112 and rev 3.
- Saturation is the only inner-loop difference: rev 3's fb clamp saturates at 62 deg/s instead of 31 — a lane change at 20 m/s runs far below either, so **this is inert for the reported symptom**; where it does act, rev 3 keeps rate feedback that V112 loses (more damping, not less).

## 5. What this settles and what it does not

- **Settled [EVIDENCE]:** the ×2 map is not an EPS-PID loop-gain change. dT/d(rate) is byte-for-byte the same at every idx and frequency; Kp is indexed by cmd, not by sp or E; the map does not enter the feedback gain at all. The ×2 lands entirely on the **cmd → wheel-rate** gain, i.e. on openpilot's loop, exactly 2.00 at every idx below saturation, with **no phase change**. V280 is 1.9–3.6× on the same path (slope ratio 3.61 above idx 24).
- **Settled [EVIDENCE]:** rev 3 differs from V112 by more than the brief listed — V268's base-assist rate-lane/boost flatten in all 34 modes (~900 B at `0xCE5BE–0xD9922`) is on the car and absent from V112. The operator's V112-vs-rev-3 comparison has two independent deltas, not one.
- **Not settled [BELIEF]:** which of the two deltas produces the lane-change oscillation. The ×2 outer-loop gain is the more direct candidate (a speed-dependent symptom is what an outer loop through v²-scaled lateral-accel gives; the inner loop has no speed term here). A plant identification from rev 3's own tap (`T_meas` on 427 vs 0x18F rate on r31, and on the lane-change route) would turn the ratios above into absolute margins; nothing in `studies/` or `memory/` holds one.
- **Implication for V280 rev 2:** it is 2.9–3.6× V112 on the same outer path in idx 24–58 (vs rev 3's 2.0×). If the lane-change ring is an outer-loop-gain effect of the ×2, V280 is further in the same direction; the inner loop is unchanged either way.
