  NOT COMPUTABLE from measured data, and I decline to manufacture it:
   * a full closed-loop |T(f)| curve  -- section 0: the two models disagree and the ring
     lives in a lane the Nyquist model's forward path does not contain.
   * f_-3dB (closed-loop bandwidth)   -- needs the plant MAGNITUDE above ~5 Hz.  The record
     measures the plant PHASE (-28 deg @10 Hz, -73 deg @22 Hz) and its DC gain, never its
     magnitude in between.  Any f_-3dB I printed would be an artefact of assuming flat.
   * max|T| over 0-50 Hz             -- the 20 Hz creep line has NO measured |L| at all
     (CREEP-20HZ item 7(a): L_in(line) = -1 by construction at a spectral line), so its
     peak height is unquantified in both directions.
  WHAT WOULD MAKE THEM COMPUTABLE: a plant MAGNITUDE identification -- a swept or
  broadband excitation with the 427 T tap and the 0x18F rate read simultaneously.  That is a
  drive design, not a build, and it is the single measurement that would turn this whole
  framework from composed to identified.
