  limiter hard-ON => pedestal carries the AC and the lane rotates to angle -76.9 deg.
  A mixture with ON-duty d would rotate the SOLVED complex `a` away from pure-real.

      regime mixture is.  It is NOT a measurement of the ROM map slope, and it should never
      be quoted as 'we measured a'.
