## 9. What decides the next lever

- The excitation V288 was built to soften (capped-command steps into the rate loop) is still what rings the 20 Hz mode, at the
  same frequency and at least the same size, on the first drive. The ÷16 D-kick on a **step** does not reach a ring that is
  sustained by 3–7 capped frames per 100 ms (mark 2) or rung by a 3 k command reversal over 1.2 s (mark 3) — those are
  low-frequency inputs the 10.3 Hz pre-filter passes almost unchanged [BELIEF, from the filter's own corner].
- The 7–8 Hz companion at ~1000 raw with hands on at full lock is the largest torque ripple in every bookmarked window, on
  V282 as on V288. Which of the two the operator calls "grinding" is his to say; both are present before every press.
- The full-lock command swing (§6) is the only new-looking thing; confounded, needs a matched check.
