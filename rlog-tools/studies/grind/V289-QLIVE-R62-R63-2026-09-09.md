## 2. b7 at grinding onsets (the build's second pre-registered read)

Episodes = 18–22 Hz envelope of the 0x18F bar ≥ 3× its engaged median for ≥ 0.3 s: r62 70 episodes (34 s), r63 45 (19 s).
b7 rises from 0.146 / 0.163 on quiet frames to 0.28 / 0.24 at onset and 0.30 / 0.24 in the episode body (bootstrap CIs
0.28–0.31 and 0.22–0.26). The predicted grinding-window range was 0.12–0.37: **met**. The quiet-frame duty is a little
above the predicted 0.10–0.11 (0.146 / 0.163) — a small miss, not a contradiction; the emulation ran on r5e's command.

At the operator's bookmarks (one per route, pressed right after a grinding episode; both at 3–5 m/s and 70–140° of
wheel angle, i.e. slow loaded turns): in the −6..0 s window b7 = 0.113 (r62) / 0.137 (r63), bar 18–22 Hz envelope max

whatever was felt; the census/marks agents should look 6–12 s earlier as well (r63's −12..−6 s window has bar env max 80).

## 3. A new fact about the hook gate (EVIDENCE for the timing, BELIEF for the mechanism)

"Reads 1.000 while disengaged" was written assuming the notch stops the instant lateral control drops. The wire says
otherwise: after 0x18F steer-control-active (SCA) falls, **b5 keeps toggling at 41 / 38 per second for the first second
and 28 / 30 per second in seconds 1–3, then 0.0 per second beyond 3 s**; b7 climbs 0.10 → 0.15 → 0.5 → 1.000 over the
same window. A frozen FLAG cannot toggle, so **the notch cave — and therefore Honda's rate PID that it sits in — keeps
executing for ~1–3 s after SCA falls**, with a nonzero, decaying S. Belief: this is Honda's disengage fade; once S is
exactly 0, |n| ≥ |y| reads 0 ≥ 0 and b7 = 1. Consequence for the scoring agents: **do not treat SCA = 0 as "hook off"
for the first 3 s after a disengage**, and score b7 engaged-only as the handoff already says. Also: in the 1 s *before*
SCA rises b7 is 0.86 / 0.67, not 1.000 — the PID begins to form S before SCA is asserted.
