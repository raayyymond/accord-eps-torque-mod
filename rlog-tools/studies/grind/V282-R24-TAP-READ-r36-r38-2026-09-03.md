### The comparator decode, re-derived from the images by me, not taken from the build script [EVIDENCE]

All four changed halfwords are the second halfword of a `24 37 xx xx` instruction — the **same opcode/register halfword `0x3724` in every one**, so
only the gp-relative displacement moved; no length change, no new code. Displacements read little-endian and signed, `gp = 0xFEDF8000`:

| instr | operand halfword | V281 rev 3 | **V283** | role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0xC4B34` | `0xC4B36` | `946c` → `gp-0x6B94` | `9526` → **`gp-0x6ADA`** | bit 6 LHS |
| `0xC4B40` | `0xC4B42` | `b09c` → `gp-0x4F64` | `94c8` → **`gp-0x6B38`** | bit 6 RHS |
| `0xC4B62` | `0xC4B64` | `951e` → `gp-0x6AE2` | `9526` → **`gp-0x6ADA`** | bit 5 LHS |
| `0xC4B6E` | `0xC4B70` | `94da` → `gp-0x6B26` | `946c` → **`gp-0x6B94`** | bit 5 RHS |

So **bit 6 = |`gp-0x6ADA`| ≥ |`gp-0x6B38`| = |r24| ≥ |T|** and **bit 5 = |`gp-0x6ADA`| ≥ |`gp-0x6B94`| = |r24| ≥ |aggregator|**, exactly as
`PREREG-V282-READ.md` declares — and the *old* pairs are exactly the ones the record names (`|6b94| ≥ |4f64|`, duty 0.0000; `|6ae2| ≥ |6b26|`,
duty 0.337, measured 0.3363 on r34 here). Every duty below rests on this decode and it is confirmed from the bytes, not relayed.

### 3.1 Does the AC-vs-DC asymmetry that voided (D) also contaminate this? **No — tested, and the finding is strengthened** [EVIDENCE]

measured / predicted-at-5244, not the duty** — because the prediction collapses with DC too, so a falling duty proves nothing by itself.

closed form.**

Two further checks, both of which also work against the finding being an artefact:

- **Tap quantisation and T = 0 ties.** The tap quantises |T| in steps of 8, and when both operands are ~0 the rung fires. Zero-tap frames are only
  4.5 % of the low-DC bin, and excluding them moves the measured duty 0.2934 → 0.2867 (s 0.30 → 0.35). Both effects inflate the *measured* duty,
  i.e. they push s up, not down.
- **Reconstruction bandwidth.** r24's `d = 0.5·(x[n] − x[n−4])` runs at 1 kHz and passes bar content to ~125 Hz, but the closed form rebuilds x from
  the **100 Hz** CAN bar via `resample_poly(10,1)`, which band-limits it to 50 Hz. Any real bar content above 50 Hz is missing from |r24|_cf, so the
  prediction is a **lower bound**. The measured duty came in *below* that lower bound. [EVIDENCE, arithmetic]

**Verdict: the magnitude finding stands and is strengthened. |r24| on the wire is 0.30–0.47 of the closed form at the flown 5244 arm, best estimate
0.37 from 30.2 s of the cleanest AC-vs-AC frames, upper bound 0.52 at 2 sd.**

**Which part of the closed form is the suspect [BELIEF, with the arithmetic]:**

1. ⭐ **The assumed `dt` of the derivative — the leading candidate.** The closed form takes `d = 0.5·(x[n] − x[n−4])` on a **1 kHz** tick, i.e. a
   4.0 ms span. For a sinusoid at f the differencer's gain is `2·sin(π f dt)/2 = sin(π f dt)`. At 20 Hz: `dt = 4.0 ms → 0.249`, but
   `dt = 1.6 ms → 0.100` — **a factor 2.5, almost exactly the 2.7 the wire demands.** Crucially it is *also* consistent with (C): the differencer's
   phase is `90° − 180·f·dt`, i.e. **75.6° at dt = 4 ms and 84.2° at dt = 1.6 ms — an 8.6° shift, well inside my measured residual of −13…−18°.**
   A shorter effective tick (or a span of fewer than 4 samples) shrinks the magnitude by ~2.5× while barely moving the phase. That is the only single
   parameter I have found that reproduces both observations at once.
2. **The Q-format of the `>>10`/`×0.5` chain** — a one-bit error anywhere in it is a factor 2, also compatible.
3. **The gain arm** — Honda's rate LERP (2150–3072 at creep) instead of `0xC6446` = 5244 would give s ≈ 0.41–0.59, which the whole-stratum inversion
   fits but the clean low-DC cut (s = 0.37, arm ≈ 1940) fits only at its edge. **Not excluded, and `gp-0x671d` / `gp-0x683c` remain untraced.**
4. **NOT the deadband.** The `|s| ≤ 3 → 0` deadband removes ~3 of a p50 21–28 counts in creep — ~12 %, an order of magnitude short of the gap.
5. **NOT reconstruction bandwidth** — that biases the prediction *down*, the wrong way (§3.1).

**What would settle it:** a trace of the actual tick rate and sample span feeding `gp-0x4f62` at `FUN_0003aa2c`, and the `gp-0x671d` / `gp-0x683c`
reads. Both are Ghidra work on the image, not another drive.

## 7. Open items and what would falsify this read

1. **`gp-0x671d` and `gp-0x683c` are still untraced on the wire.** They are the difference between "the 5244 arm is live and the closed form's
   scale is 2.3× wrong" and "Honda's 2150–3072 LERP arm is live". s = 0.43 is compatible with both and does not choose. The fired decision
   sentence names this trace; it remains the right next step even though its "1024 arm" premise is not supported.
2. **A duty threshold registered as an absolute number does not transfer between route sets.** The prereg's 0.300 became 0.152 on these routes'
   own data — a 2× shift from the operating point alone. Register the next one as a ratio to the same drive's replay.
3. **The `dt` suspect in §3.2 is a hypothesis, not a trace.** A ~2.5× shorter effective derivative span reproduces both the magnitude gap and the
   small phase residual, but I have not read the tick rate or the sample span feeding `gp-0x4f62` out of `FUN_0003aa2c`. That is Ghidra work on the
   image and it would settle §3.2 item 1 against item 3. **What would falsify the rescale as a whole:** an error in the closed form that is *not* a
   pure gain at 7–22 Hz — the phase agreement (−13…−18°, replicated on three builds) currently argues against that, but only to ~±15°.
4. **A comparator rung is only informative where both operands carry comparable DC.** That is the general form of (D)'s defect, and it is now also a
   quantified bound on (A): the useful frames are the low-|T|_DC ones, of which V283 gave 30.2 s. A future rung wanting a 7 Hz answer must compare
   AC to AC (`|r24| ≥ |T − LPF(T)|`, or `|r24| ≥ |P|`) — a 10-byte displacement change of the class V282 already proved buildable.
