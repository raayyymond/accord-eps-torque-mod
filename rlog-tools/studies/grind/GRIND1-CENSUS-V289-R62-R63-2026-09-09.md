2. The amplitude of the line has grown sharply: amp max 263 (r62) / **450** (r63) vs 164-207 on the
   V282/V288 comparators — a real increase, not a detection-threshold artifact (the raw envelope, not just
   presence-crossing count, is larger).
3. Presence has risen from 9-27 % (V282/V288 spread) to **24.6-35.0 %** (V289).
4. Hands-off/creep stratification (§4b of the agnostic file) shows the V289 14-17 Hz share is now dominant
   specifically at **< 8 m/s** (r62_v289 34 %, r63_v289 51 % of hands-off creep windows) and **8-18 m/s**
   (24/34 %), while at > 18 m/s it FALLS to 1-3 % — the opposite of r3a/r3c, whose 14-17 Hz share is highest
   at high speed (24-33 %). **The V289 line and the pre-existing V282/V288 14-17 Hz companion are not the
   same population by speed dependence.**

**Two-lines-at-once check** (§4d): on V289 routes, windows with BOTH a 14-17 AND an 18-22 Hz peak above
threshold simultaneously are rare (4.1 % of present windows on both r62/r63, similar to the 2.3-3.8 % seen
on every V282/V288 route) — there is no evidence of two co-existing lines; it reads as one line that has
moved.

**Verdict [BELIEF, built on the EVIDENCE above]: SHIFTED, not a separately unmasked pre-existing mode.**
A weak ~14.3 Hz companion line existed on every comparator route at low amplitude and modest, speed-agnostic
presence. On V289 the line has moved up to ~15.2-15.3 Hz, grown 1.5-2× in amplitude, more than doubled in
presence, and become concentrated at exactly the low/mid-speed regime where the notch's own crossover-pole
relocation was already predicted (`docs/review/ADV-V290-PHYSICS-2026-09-09.md`: 19.87 Hz ζ+0.033 →
16.32 Hz ζ−0.016, a marginally-unstable limit cycle). This census's numbers are consistent with that
mechanism; they do not independently establish it.
