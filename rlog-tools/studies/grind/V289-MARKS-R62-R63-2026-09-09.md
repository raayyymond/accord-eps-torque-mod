x. The tap T is on its native 50 Hz instants; the rate is band-limited 15–26 Hz before being sampled at T's instants (nothing above
30 Hz can alias onto the line). The record's convention: sign(T) ≠ sign(rate) ⇒ damping, so 180° = damping, 0° = pumping.
