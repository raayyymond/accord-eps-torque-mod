## 1. Method, convention, and the positive control

### 1.1 Convention — stated once, and it is convention-free by construction

```
bar  = 0x18F bytes 0-1, as cache/v280 decodes it, x 1.024      (raw counts)
rate = 0x18F bytes 2-3, as cache/v280 decodes it               (raw counts, 8 per deg/s)
B   := bar / rate,  NO negation applied to either signal
```
`gp-0x4f60 = −bar` and `gp-0x6a56 = −rate` are negated by the **same** frame builder, so **the ratio is
identical in wire sign and internal sign**. With that `B`,

```
R_r24(f) = −(gain/1024)·D4(f)·B(f)        D4(f) = 0.5·(1 − e^{−j2πf·0.004})
```
is the phase of the **cell `gp-0x6ada`** per unit of `x = −rate` (the internal rate), i.e. exactly your
convention, and **positive real part = damping**. Every phase in this document is in that reference
unless the column header says `|wire`, which is the same number minus 180°.

> 🛑 **The 180° trap, re-confirmed here.** `cache/v280` decodes 0x18F torque **and** rate with the
> opposite sign to `_scratch/r24sign_*.npz` (recorded in `V282-R24-TAP-READ-r36-r38-2026-09-03.md`
> §0.5). `B` is immune — both flip. **`r24_series(g["bar"], gain)` applied to the v280 cache is not**:
> it returns the cell **negated**. My first pass had the timing control reading +179° instead of 0°
> for exactly this reason. The fix is one explicit negation, documented in the script; it is **not** a
> free parameter, and with it the control reads 0…−13°. **Your closed form is unaffected** — `B` is a
> ratio — but anything you do in the time domain with `r24_series` on that cache is.

### 1.2 Estimator

Pooled Welch cross-spectra over contiguous runs inside each stratum (the record's own `Pool`, so the
numbers are comparable): `nperseg 128`, Hann, 50 % overlap, `detrend="constant"`, spectra interpolated
to 0.195 Hz (`nfft 512`), then **band-averaged over `f0 ± 0.40 Hz`** — so every row is evaluated **at**
the requested frequency, not at a neighbouring bin. Cross-check at `nperseg 256` / `nfft 1024`; the two
agree to ≤ 0.1 in `|B|` and ≤ 5° in phase wherever both have windows.

**Three magnitude estimates, because the input is noisy:**

| | formula | bias |
|---|---|---|
| `H1` | `Sxy/Sxx` | biased **LOW** — this is what the record quotes |
| `H2` | `Syy/Syx` | biased **HIGH** |
| `Hv` | `H1/√coh` (their geometric mean) | **recommended central value** |

Their spread **is** the magnitude uncertainty. Phase is identical for all three. Ignoring this was
producing a fake `f^-1.8` roll-off above 20 Hz: at coherence 0.53 `H1` is low by ~1.4×.

**`coh < 0.40` ⇒ UNUSABLE.** No phase is reported for those points and `Hv` is unreliable there
(the correction over-fires when the coherence loss is phase smearing, not additive noise — which is
what the 10–14 Hz hole is).

## 7. Check (4) — the `gp-0x671d` first-strike latch: a clean absence, three ways

**Primary statistic**: `ratio = duty_measured / duty_predicted_at_5244` on the same frames — this
divides out the regime dependence that makes the raw duty useless. Windows of **30 s of engaged-lateral
time**, 15 s step, in route order.

**No monotone step-down on either route.** Both routes *drift slightly UP* over the drive (the best

regime drift, not a gain change.

**Detection sensitivity, stated plainly:** the duty is locally `∝ gain^1.015` near the operating point,
so the 0.031 floor corresponds to a **~6 % persistent drop in the effective r24 gain**. The
hypothesised latch is a **80.5 % drop**. It is **13× the detection floor** and would have been
unmissable.

**Two independent bounds, in case the latch fired before the first engaged window:**

1. **Absolute level.** If the latch had been set from the start, the implied arm would read
   ≈ 5244 × 0.195 = **1023**. It reads **2268–2747** in every stratum on both routes. A latched drive
   is excluded in absolute terms, not just by the absence of a step.
2. 🛑 **The SET threshold is never reached.** Over 4653 s of driving:

## 8. What I did NOT verify

1. **The r6c → V282 attribution.** I took `ROUTE-6C-ATTRIBUTION-2026-09-13.md` as given. I did
   observe, independently, that r6c's byte-4 bits 0–2 are 0b111 on all 370 246 frames and that its
   bit-6 duty and implied arm sit exactly on r39's V282 values — **consistent with V282, not a
   re-derivation.**
2. **The `gp-0x671d` Schmitt latch itself** — its existence, its input, its 5530/1024 thresholds, its
   ×0.195 factor. All of that is the sibling tracer's; I bounded its *consequences* on the wire only.
3. **Whether the 0.44× magnitude gap is the arm or the mirror's dt/scale.** I report the ratio, which
   is what the wire measures. Resolving it needs an AC-vs-AC comparator rung (`|r24| ≥ |T − LPF(T)|`),
   already identified in the record as a 10-byte displacement edit.
4. **`B(f)` below 5 Hz, at 10–14 Hz, and above 25 Hz** — not identified at the required coherence in
   any stratum. The fit covers those bands by extrapolation; `is_usable()` says so.
5. **Any causal claim about `B`.** `B` is a measured *ratio of two logged signals on the same frame*.
   It is not proven to be a plant transfer, and both signals sit inside a closed loop — the estimate
   is a closed-loop ratio, so it can be biased by feedback correlated with the bar. I did **not** run
   an instrumental-variable (e.g. `cmd`-IV) version; `creep20_loop_id.py` has that machinery if
   `r24lane` wants the crux checked.
6. **The `0x18F` decode's absolute sign** against the DBC or the firmware. Everything here is anchored
   to `cache/v280`'s decode and to the record's own +114° number, which I reproduced. The *ratio* `B`
   is immune; the *sign* work in §6 is anchored by the timing control, not by an assumed polarity.
7. **`|T|`'s calibration** (the 427 tap). Used as-is for the comparator replay.
8. **The `nperseg`-256 cross-check for the thin strata** (`loaded_idx68` on r6c had 0 windows at 256 in
   the first pass). The 128/512 primary is what the tables report.
