## 7. Caveats, and what I would do next

- 🛑 **Frequency labels above ~25 Hz are not claimed anywhere in this file.** Per
  `TASK5-RATE-AND-ALIAS-2026-09-04.md`, there is no anti-alias filter in the path, so a line labelled
  20 Hz could in principle be 80 Hz folded. Everything above is either an **energy/band** statement
  (safe) or a line at **7–21 Hz**, well inside the 50 Hz Nyquist of the de-jittered `0x18F` stream. The
  24–28 Hz control band is reported alongside every core so a broadband rise would be visible.
- 🛑 **No 20 Hz loop-gain or margin figure is produced**, per `CREEP-20HZ-LOOP-ID` §7(a):
  `L_in(line) = −1 by construction` at a spectral line, so any such number would be tautological.
- 🛑 **The withdrawn ≤ 22–33 % folded-fraction bound is not cited.**
- ⚠ **The confound is real and is not fully removed.** r39 is V282 **plus** a 1.70× outer loop. The
  idx-reweight removes the exposure share; it cannot remove the bandwidth share. **One drive of V282 at
  the OLD tune (SR 12.5, `SteerKP` 0.6) would separate them, costs no firmware change, and would make
  every r35 → V282 contrast in §3 clean.** That is my single recommendation from this read.
- ⚠ **Two bookmarks is a small supervised sample.** §3.4 shows there was a **louder** 20 Hz stretch he
  did not mark (t 883.2), so the marks are a sample of what he *noticed*, not of what happened.
- ⚠ **The Kp attribution rests on one demand-index bin** (§1). It separates r39 from r34 cleanly but does
  not exercise the "must rise across the bins" test.
- 🛑 **Reported for correction, not acted on:** dec39's `r39_1ab.json` 427-tap descriptor
  (`sar0` / `0.2000 counts per LSB`) contradicts the wire; see §0.10.

**What I did NOT do, and would flag as unowned:** the operator's over-steer report (*"worst-case
