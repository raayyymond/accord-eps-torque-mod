## Headline

1. **The cave is LIVE and its arithmetic matches the mirror** [EVIDENCE]. 0x14A b4.5 agrees with sign(y_model) on **99.50 %** of engaged frames (best frame phase +2 ms; 99.34 % at 0). Every engaged segment has bit-5 duty strictly inside (0,1) — 0.14 … 0.56 — so the rung executes everywhere; nothing says the build is not V288 rev 2 (§6).
2. **The measured zero-crossing lag is ~3 ms, not 15 ms — and that is what the built arithmetic predicts** [EVIDENCE]. bit-5 edges trail sign(sp_raw) by median **+10.2 ms [95 % CI 9.9 … 10.4]** and sign(y_model) by **+7.4 ms [7.2 … 7.7]**; the difference **2.7 ms** is the filter's own contribution, and the mirror alone gives 3.0 ms. Cross-correlation on the 1 kHz grid peaks at +6 ms [block-bootstrap CI 5 … 7]. The 7.4 ms residual is frame phase + bus latency + the 5 ms mean edge-quantisation of a 100 Hz sampler. **The pre-registered "≈ 15 ms at zero crossings" was a mis-specified expectation, not a miss by the cave** — see 3.
3. **The +1 rounding fix makes the cave a unit-slew follower below 16 counts of tracking error** [EVIDENCE: arithmetic + this route]. `sar` floors and the fix forces |Δy| = 1 for any 0 < |sp − y| < 16, so small signals are tracked at 1 count/ms with ~1 count of lag — no pole, no group delay. Consequences measured on this drive: |sp_raw − y| is **0 on 66 % of engaged ticks**, ≥ 16 (the linear-pole regime) on **10.7 %**, ≥ 32 on 4.4 %. Delivered 18–22 Hz attenuation of sp: **|H| = 0.56 overall** (linear 10.3 Hz pole: 0.46); per 2 s window it is **0.93 when p90 |sp − y| < 8 counts (210 of 315 windows), 0.65 at 8–32, 0.47 at ≥ 32**. At 30–45 Hz: 0.57 vs 0.26 linear. **The small 20 Hz echo on the command passes the filter nearly untouched; only the large capped-frame steps are spread out.**
4. **The "kick ÷ 16" holds as a ratio (11.3× p50 on capped frames), but the design note's absolute numbers were in the wrong units** [EVIDENCE]. A capped 123-raw step is ~33 sp counts (map slope 4.3/idx, idx = raw/16), i.e. 32·33 = 1056 — measured p50 **1088 → 96**. "3936 → 224" treated raw 0xE4 counts as sp counts. The image's D clamp is **10240** (binds at |ΔE| ≥ 640 = |Δy| ≥ 20/tick), so the filtered steps never bind D; the integrated D per capped frame is conserved (+8 %). Open-loop mirror: 18–22 Hz amplitude of T 31.4 → 26.9 (−14 %), of T_D 26.7 → 23.5, of T_P 15.8 → 12.6.
5. **No new 1–3 Hz wallow** [descriptive, one route per arm]. Straight hands-off strata, ratio V288 / pooled V282 (r39+r3a+r3c) of 1–3 Hz power: angle 0.65 / 0.64 / 0.91 (v 1–6 / 6–15 / 15–30 m/s), command 0.86 / 1.04 / 0.99, bar 0.45 / 1.05 / 0.71 — CIs in §5, none above 1 for angle. openpilot's `co_req` 1–3 Hz is at or below the V282 routes in every stratum.
6. **The grind persisted with the filter demonstrably acting** [EVIDENCE for the filter state; the symptom is the operator's]. In ±2 s of bookmark 1 the command sat on the slew cap on **65 %** of frames and the filter lagged **26 counts** p50 (its linear regime); bookmark 2: 27 % capped, lag 10; bookmark 3: 10 %, lag 4 (small-signal regime). A null on grinding here is not "the filter did not run".

## Corrections of record proposed
1. "bit-5 zero crossings vs sign(sp_raw) read the ≈ 15 ms group delay" — false for the built arithmetic: crossings happen in the unit-slew regime and read ~3 ms. The 15 ms applies only when |sp − y| ≥ 16 (10.7 % of engaged ticks here).
2. "ΔE 3936 → 224 per tick on a capped step" — units: 1088 → 96 measured (raw p50 on capped frames); ratio 11×.
3. "the D clamp binds at 640" — 640 is in ΔE units; the cell is 10240 in D units and the filtered steps never reach it.

## Open
- The filter's 20 Hz attenuation is amplitude-dependent (0.93 → 0.47). If the echo is the target, K = 4 with the +1 fix does not touch it; a linear form needs a different rounding (e.g. carry the fractional part in a wider state), which is a new cave, not a re-cut.
- Hook-run condition modelled as engaged-lateral: 0.5 % disagreement, none edge-related — adequate for liveness, not a proof of the ramp semantics.
