## 1. Method

- **Caches:** `analysis-2020accord/_scratch/cache/v280/{r39,r3a,r3c,r35,r36,r37,r38}.npz` (+ `_b4.npz`
  cave-byte caches), loaded via `creep20_loop_id.load()` — nothing re-extracted. `eng` = LATERAL
  engaged (0x18F `STEER_CONTROL_ACTIVE` AND 0xE4 `STEER_REQUEST`, src 129), gap frames excluded
  because `have18` gates `eng` (verified: r3a's ~60 s segment-10 hole produces zero episodes spanning
  it — the gap frames are simply never engaged).
- **Cells / idx:** `grind_incident_r35.read_cells` / `demand_live` reused verbatim, on the V282 image
  (`_v282_V282-V281R3BASE-KP.FLAT.Y0-CAVE.R24CMP.BITS5.6-MAP.LINEAR.TO6X.FEEDBACK46080.TORQUE.TAP_plain_image.bin`)
  for r39/r3a/r3c, the V281r3 image for r35, the V283 image for r36-r38.
- **Presence census:** 2 s windows, 0.5 s step, engaged; "present" = most prominent 15-26 Hz peak has
  prominence ≥ 8 AND bar 18-22 Hz band amplitude ≥ 40 raw — `v283_grind_census.py`'s threshold,
  unchanged, so every build reads on the same yardstick.
- **Episode:** a contiguous run ≥ 0.5 s (inside an engaged run) whose nearest 2 s census window is
  "present". Per episode: Hilbert envelope (18-22 Hz bandpass) over an extended context window,
  `grind_incident_r35.growth_fit`'s log-envelope rise/decay slopes.
- **Classification [BELIEF on the boundary, EVIDENCE on the numbers feeding it]:**
  - **BURST** — rise ≥ +1.0 /s AND decay ≤ -1.0 /s (r35's own incident: +1.42/-3.90 /s) AND duration
    ≤ 3.0 s.
  - **RIDE-ALONG** — bar 6-10 Hz concurrent amplitude ≥ 1.2× the 18-22 Hz amplitude AND the two bands'
    0.25 s envelopes correlate ≥ 0.5 (the r39 bookmark class — 20 Hz riding under a 7 Hz ring).
  - **SUSTAINED** — everything else.
- **Transient predicate** at onset t0: |dθ/dt| > 30°/s, OR d(idx)/dt > 40/s, OR |rate| > 30°/s, each
  1 s-smoothed, evaluated anywhere in [t0-1.5, t0+1.5]. **Steady creep** at onset: v ∈ [1,3) m/s,
  hands off (|bar| < 400), |rate| < 10°/s AT t0 itself.
- **Enrichment:** episode-onset P(transient) / baseline P(transient), baseline drawn one instant per
  second of engaged time (n=1958) to avoid 100 Hz autocorrelation. Bootstrap resamples EPISODES (not
  windows), n=4000.
- **Rail check:** `grind_incident_r35.eval_window` (the chain mirror, live fade, ZOH command) on the
  three loudest V282 episodes overall.
- **Detector test:** for episodes ≥ 0.4 s, the ratio of the 18-22 Hz envelope of wheel rate (deg/s) to
  the 18-22 Hz envelope of the 427 tap T, and of median |bar| to median |T|, in the first 0.3 s of the
  episode vs the last 0.3 s vs 0.5 s AFTER the episode ends. A ≥ 25 % drop timed at a 0.3-0.65 s
  duration would be consistent with (not proof of) the detector's 375-500 ms reset-and-cut window.

## 6. Honda oscillation-reversal detector test (requested by team-lead, 2026-09-06)

Detector per the tracer: 39 Hz-filtered d(rotor angle)/dt (`gp-0x6c2c`), alternate-crossing reversals
of ±40 % full scale, only counted > 10 Hz (50 ms reset), 15-20 reversals (375-500 ms at 20 Hz) ramps a
multiplicative governor cut to ×0.600 (0xC694A: level 0/15/20/25 → 1.0/1.0/0.6/0.6), slew-limited. The
427 tap T is upstream of the cut, so a firing detector should show delivered motion per unit T falling
~0.6× late in an episode, timed 0.375-0.5 s after onset.

**Test result [EVIDENCE against a clean signature; NOT resolved either way]:**

- **39 / 281 episodes ≥ 0.4 s (14 %)** show a ≥ 25 % drop in the 18-22 Hz rate/T envelope ratio or the
  quasi-static |bar|/|T| ratio, timed at a 0.3-0.65 s duration. This is a loose OR-of-two-ratios screen
  with no hand verification — treat these as **unverified candidates**, not confirmed detector hits.
- **Duration histogram** (all 281 episodes, all routes) shows no clean pile-up at 0.4-0.6 s that isn't
  explained by the census's own detection floor:

  | duration bin | n | % |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0.5-0.6 s | 74 | 26 % |
  | 0.6-0.8 s | 0 | 0 % |
  | 0.8-1.0 s | 10 | 4 % |
  | 1.0-1.5 s | 42 | 15 % |
  | 1.5-2.5 s | 67 | 24 % |
  | 2.5+ s | 88 | 31 % |

  🛑 **Confound, not a finding:** episodes cannot be shorter than 0.5 s by construction (the census's
  own minimum-duration cut), so the 0.5-0.6 s bin is structurally inflated regardless of any detector
  behaviour. The 0.6-0.8 s bin reading exactly 0 (an artifact of the 0.5 s step / rounding in episode
  boundary detection) makes this histogram unfit to test the detector's 375-500 ms signature as built —
  **a re-run with a finer, un-quantised episode boundary would be needed to use duration as evidence.**
- **Direct test on the r35 23:48:21 incident** (rise 1016.3-1017.2, decay to ~1017.65): the delivered-
  response ratios are **contradictory** — bar/T ratio drops 27 % (0.73×) after the collapse, consistent
  with a cut, but rate/T ratio RISES 44 % (1.44×) over the same window, inconsistent with one. The
  study's own account (`GRIND-INCIDENT-r35-2026-09-03.md`) already attributes the collapse to the
  driver's hands tightening (|tq| 270 → 1200 raw a full second before the 2500-raw grab) — this test
  cannot separate a detector cut layered under that from the hands alone.

**Bottom line:** no episode in this set cleanly confirms the oscillation-reversal detector firing.
Whether it participates in grind #1 is **NOT resolved by this census** — the ratio-based proxy is too
indirect and the duration test is confounded by the detector's own methodology. **Next step, if this
question matters:** tap `gp-0x6c2c`'s reversal count or the 0xC694A ramp-level cell directly and re-run
against the same episode list; that is a direct read, not an inference from T/rate/bar ratios.

## 6b. Sharper onset predicate — top-1% |Δcmd| / Δidx ticks (added 2026-09-06, requested by team-lead)

Per `GRIND1-LOOP-SHAPE-V287-2026-09-06.md` Appendix B3, the D-clamp's own predicate is sharper than
§4's loose transient flag: **"onset = the 0.5 s after a |Δsp| in its top 1 % of non-zero steps."** Here
that is operationalised on the logged 0xE4 command and on `idx` directly: a "big tick" frame is
`|Δcmd| ≥` its pooled-V282 99th percentile among nonzero steps (122 raw/frame) **OR** `Δidx ≥` its
99th percentile (9.0/frame). An episode onset is "near a tick" if a big-tick frame occurs anywhere in
the ±0.5 s window around it (team-lead's phrasing; the appendix's own version is one-sided, 0.5 s
*after* the tick — both give the same qualitative read here).

| class | n | P(near tick) | enrichment vs baseline | 95% CI (bootstrap, resample episodes) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BURST | 48 | 0.23 | 2.09× | [1.14, 3.23]× |
| SUSTAINED | 51 | 0.41 | 3.75× | [2.50, 5.00]× |
| RIDE-ALONG | 31 | 0.26 | 2.35× | [1.18, 3.82]× |
| **ALL (V282 pooled)** | **130** | **0.31** | **2.80×** | **[2.10, 3.57]×** |

Baseline: P(near a top-1 % tick) over 1 Hz-sampled V282 engaged frames (n=1958) = **0.110**.

**Reading [EVIDENCE for the numbers, BELIEF for the interpretation]:** this predicate is a real
improvement on §4's loose transient flag — the baseline rate drops from 79 % to 11 %, so a 2.80×
pooled enrichment (and up to 3.75× for SUSTAINED) is a much sharper signal than the 1.25× found there.
**Command/idx steps are genuinely enriched at grind #1 onsets**, most strongly for SUSTAINED episodes,
least strongly (though still > 2×, CI excludes 1) for BURST — consistent with the D-clamp appendix's
own finding that the clamp's binding ticks are 92.6–100 % setpoint-step-dominated in exactly this kind
of episode. Still, **69 % of V282 onsets are NOT near a top-1 % step** — a command/idx step raises the
odds of an onset several-fold but is far from necessary; most episodes open without one.
