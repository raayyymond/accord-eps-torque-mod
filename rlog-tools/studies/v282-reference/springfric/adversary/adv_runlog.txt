WHAT THE DECOMPOSITION IS, ALGEBRAICALLY  (this is the whole attack surface)
  away episodes have sign(aa)=+sj, toward episodes sign(aa)=-sj.  Write
    R = cmd[bk] - K*aa[bk] - c      u = R*sign(aa)  (= y)
  Plant = spring S(theta) + Coulomb F.  Wheel stuck at theta requires the command,
  measured OUTWARD-positive, to satisfy   S(|th|) - F  <  u  <  S(|th|) + F.
  Breaking OUTWARD means u hit the UPPER edge: u = S(|th|)+F.
  Breaking INWARD  means u hit the LOWER edge: u = S(|th|)-F.
  So med_away = S - K|th| + F ,  med_toward = S - K|th| - F , hence
     CENTRE = S(|th|) - K*|th|        HALF-WIDTH = F
  => HALF-WIDTH flat is the Coulomb force.  CENTRE IS THE SPRING MISFIT, S(th)-K*th,
     WHICH IS ZERO AT th=0 BY CONSTRUCTION FOR ANY S AND ANY K.  The "454x span" is
     the span of a function that must vanish at the origin, divided by its own
     smallest bin.  It carries no information about whether S-K*th is a DEFICIT.
