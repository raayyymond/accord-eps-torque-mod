Adversarial verification of finding D5 (d_v282low__D5__attribution, lever-reach / attribution)
Verdict: REFUTED (as stated). residual_confidence 0.15 (author claimed 0.45).

D5 is explicitly a synthesis/judgement built on D1-D4, not a new measurement ("Nothing here
comes from the simulator... the x1.5-2 figure is a judgement, not a computation"). I re-read the
underlying artifacts D5 draws numbers from: d_v282low/d_analyze.out (sections A/C/D/E),
d_v282low/d_results.json, a_stickslip/ss_extract.py, a_stickslip/out*/ss_analyze.json
(the "matched" block), and the prior D2 verdict.

QUALITATIVE STRUCTURE -- SURVIVES:
- The PARTLY-REACHABLE argument (a 100 Hz torque loop carrying ~130 ms of phase lag at 2-4 Hz
  cannot fully reject a 2-2.7 Hz lightly-damped wheel mode, only damp it) is physically sound and
  its input numbers check out: cmd_to_rate lag at 2.0-4.0 Hz across all four torque builds and
  both speed bands is 114-145 ms (T64 128/134, T64B 114/141, T5 144/145, T4 123/136 ms) -- "130 ms"
  is a fair central estimate. The cited V282 wheel-mode-band levels (0.5/1.3/2.1/3.2 deg/s <8 m/s)
  reproduce exactly against d_matched.out (already CONFIRMED in the D2 verify pass). The framing
  "can at most stop exciting and add some damping, not reject" is a reasonable engineering read of
  ~120 deg of loop-delay phase at the mode frequency, and the "x1.5-2" figure is honestly flagged
  as a judgement call, not something I can independently falsify -- it is not refuted.

TWO LOAD-BEARING NUMERIC PROBLEMS FOUND -- REFUTE THE FINDING AS WRITTEN:

1. The REACHABLE tier's own justification cherry-picks the favorable speed cell.
   - rate_ratio: D5 says "V282 0.84-0.94; torque over-drives at 1.15-1.9" and sets the reachable
     target at "<=~1.0". But d_analyze.out section E shows V282's OWN rate_ratio median is 0.842 at
     2.5-8 m/s (matches) yet 1.061 at 8-15 m/s (n=4, IQR [1.026,1.097]) -- V282 itself exceeds the
     claimed "<=1.0" ceiling in that band. V282old is worse still: 0.936 (2.5-8) vs 1.093 (8-15,
     n=6). So "match V282" does not actually imply "<=1.0" once both speed bands are counted; the
     8-15 m/s cell was left out of the citation.
   - overrun: D5's "<=~4 deg" target is grounded in V282's 2.5-8 m/s median (4.089 deg, matches),
     but V282old's OWN 8-15 m/s median overrun is 9.752 deg (n=6) -- more than double the claimed
     target and higher than several torque-mode cells (T4 4.449, T64B 5.089). The reference class
     does not uniformly clear the bar D5 sets for it.
   - des_to_angle lag at 0.3-1.0 Hz: D5's "no loss of the current 0.04-0.10 s" undercounts; T64 and
     T5 both read 0.131-0.132 s in the 8-15 m/s cell, 32% over the stated 0.10 s ceiling.
   These are all n=3-11 event counts (small-sample), which D5 does not flag for this tier the way
   it flags small-n for the dwell-jump tier -- the REACHABLE tier is presented as more solid than
   its own inputs support.

2. The UNCERTAIN tier's actionable number, "dither acting faster than the dwell (at or under
   0.12 s)," is NOT derived from any measured dwell-duration statistic. It traces to
   a_stickslip/ss_extract.py line 19: `DWMIN = os.environ.get('DWMIN','20') # 20 = 0.20 s
   (primary), 12 = the s4 0.12 s sensitivity` -- a minimum-duration THRESHOLD used to test whether
   the dwell-then-jump census is robust to a stricter (shorter) qualifying window, not a measured
   or derived physical dwell timescale. The actual measured median dwell durations, from
   a_stickslip/out/ss_analyze.json's "matched" block, are 0.30-0.54 s on both classes: dwell_p50
   torque 0.53 (T64) / 0.36 (T64B) / 0.53 (T4) s, dwell_p50 V282 0.36 / 0.30 / 0.30 s. The dwell
   detector's own primary definition additionally requires dwells of >= 0.20 s to be counted at
   all, so nothing in this dataset shows a 0.12 s dwell. "0.12 s" appears to have been picked up
   from the wrong place (a same-named-adjacent robustness parameter) and repackaged as an
   engineering requirement; the real number, if one wants "dither faster than the shortest typical
   dwell," is close to 0.20-0.30 s -- 2 to 2.5x longer than what D5 states. This materially changes
   what "untested, not falsified" would mean for a future dither design (a dither period near
   0.12 s / ~8 Hz+ is a much harder ask than one near 0.2-0.3 s / ~3-5 Hz).

No simpler alternative explanation for the operator's complaints was uncovered; this verification
only concerns whether D5's specific reachability numbers are correctly sourced, not whether the
underlying D1-D4 facts it builds on are right (those were separately reviewed and CONFIRMED).

CORRECTED, for the record:
- rate_ratio reachable target: V282 itself spans 0.84 (2.5-8 m/s) to ~1.06-1.09 (8-15 m/s, n=4-6,
  small sample) -- "<=1.0" is optimistic for the 8-15 m/s band; a target of "<=~1.05-1.1" there
  would better match what the reference class itself achieves.
- overrun reachable target: V282-class spans ~4 deg (V282, 2.5-8 m/s) to ~9.8 deg (V282old,
  8-15 m/s, n=6) -- "<=~4 deg" only characterizes the best cell, not the reference class as a
  whole.
- the UNCERTAIN tier's dither/friction-comp timing figure should read "at or under roughly
  0.2-0.3 s" (the measured median dwell duration, 0.30-0.54 s torque / 0.30-0.36 s V282, and the
  detector's own 0.20 s minimum-dwell floor), not "0.12 s" -- that number belongs to an unrelated
  sensitivity-analysis parameter and should not be relayed as a design target.
