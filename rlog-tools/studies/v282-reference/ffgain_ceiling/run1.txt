  the >=8 m/s rows above at gain 0.5 are then unchanged; only the 2-8 m/s rows move.
  NOTE the discontinuity this creates: the gain steps at 8 m/s, so the move term jumps by
  (g-0.5)*angle_des_rate/G(8) = (g-0.5)*angle_des_rate/438 torque when the speed crosses 8 m/s.
  At 100 deg/s of desired-angle rate and g=2.0 that is a 0.34 torque step. A speed-interpolated
  schedule, not a step, is the only form of this lever that does not add its own transient.
