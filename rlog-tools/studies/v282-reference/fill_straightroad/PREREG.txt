SIGNALS (10 Hz resampled, inside straight runs)
  y     car offset LEFT of lane centre, m      = -(yL(0)+yR(0))/2 from modelV2 laneLines[1],[2] (device y is +right)
  psi   car heading LEFT relative to the lane   = slope of lane centre between x=0 and x=20 m (device frame)
  kd    model desired curvature (controlsState.desiredCurvature), a_d = kd v^2
  ka    achieved lateral accel la_pose (sign-checked against a_d)
  e     tracking error a - a_d
  I     fork integrator (torqueState.i); DOB = starpilotLateralState.accordObserverTorque (rev 5 / 6.4 only)
  y_dem = band-passed double integral of a_d; y_err = band-passed double integral of e   (y_dem + y_err ~ y, minus road)

KINEMATIC FACT used by every prediction: y'' = a - v^2 kappa_road. On a straight, y lags a by exactly 180 deg.
  CONSEQUENCE (derived, not measured): the phase of kd relative to y is -(180 - phi_track) whenever kd is tracked
  with lag phi_track, WHETHER kd is exogenous model noise OR the planner reacting to y (kd = -Ky y - Kpsi psi also
  lies in (-180,-90)). The brief's "demand lags offset" phase test is therefore NOT identifying in closed loop at one
  frequency. It is reported, with this caveat, and the discriminators below are used instead.

PREDICTIONS
 (a) DEMAND-DRIVEN (model / planner produces the weave, car tracks it):
     a_d band rms on straights larger on torque than V282 at matched speed; kd->a |H| ~ 1.0, coherence high;
     y_dem carries >~70% of y band power, y_err small; e band rms small relative to a_d.
     Planner-in-loop sub-case additionally needs y (offset) larger with the same planner, i.e. kd tracks y.
 (b) TRACKING ERROR (fork over-gain + lag): |H(a_d -> a)| in 0.08-0.25 Hz departs from 1 (over-delivery >1.1 or
     lag >~20 deg) on torque but not V282; e band rms torque/V282 > 1; y_err is a large share of y;
     coherence a_d->a still high (it is a linear defect).
 (c) LIMIT CYCLE (integrator/DOB vs Coulomb friction): weave persists in the LOW-DEMAND half of straights
     (a_d band rms below median) with a narrow spectral line in a / y; coherence a_d->a LOW (<0.5) in band;
     I or DOB band rms large with sign reversals synchronised to the angle reversals (I/DOB leading angle by
     ~90 deg, an integrator of the error); torque oscillation amplitude comparable to the friction level
     (plant F ~0.011 output units; fork hysteresis F 0.015).
 NULL: torque/V282 ratio of y band rms (and a band rms) with a route-cluster bootstrap 95% CI containing 1.0 and
     the point within the MDE. MDE (80% power, alpha 0.05) = 2.8 x SE(log ratio) from the route bootstrap; with 3
     V282 routes vs 5 torque routes, expected MDE ~ x1.5-2 -- a smaller effect cannot be excluded by these data.
