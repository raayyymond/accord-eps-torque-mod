## 3. Is openpilot in the loop? — its command carries the line; what changed is the EPS's command gain (EVIDENCE + BELIEF, marked)
- **The 0xE4 command rings at the line, coherence 1.00 with the rate in all nine episodes and 0.87–0.96 over the six ringing lane-change
  windows**, 4–12 Hz amplitude 105–223 counts (25–40 % of the excursion's peak), phase +17…+49° re the raw rate, same sign as T. In the
  operator's example 1 the command's own ±10 s spectrum peaks at 7.42 Hz. controlsState.torqueState: desiredLateralAccel 4–12 Hz amplitude
  0.01–0.07 m/s² (the planner is flat); **error rings 0.05–0.07 m/s²** — openpilot's measurement sees the wheel ring and the command answers.
  This is NOT "command flat, rate/T ringing" (r31's high-angle picture, command railed at 4096).
- **openpilot's block is the SAME on every route.** H1 cmd/angle at 7.8 Hz, highway frames: r32 866 counts/deg ∠−66° (coh 0.94), r33 875
  ∠−69° (0.93), **r22 V112 635 ∠−62° (0.92), r97 stock 792 ∠−59° (0.82)**; in the episodes 680–740 ∠−75°. carParams torque tune identical on
  all five routes (friction 0.212, latAccelFactor 1.689, steerActuatorDelay 0.10); r31/r32/r33 = the same openpilot commit `75577ecf`
  ("Dom 0.11.2"); r22 `2c002755`, r97 `a0cf2853`. openpilot did not change between the smooth and the oscillating drives.
- **The EPS block is what differs.** H1 rate/cmd at 7.8 Hz: **r32 0.27, r33 0.29 wire/count (coh 0.83–0.88; 0.38–0.47 in the episodes, coh
  0.99)** vs V112 0.23 (coh 0.63) vs stock 0.11 (coh 0.35). ⚠ closed-loop H1 is biased where coherence is low. (The three-block product in
  `LANECHANGE-loop.txt` is the coherence product around a closed cycle, NOT a loop gain; ignore that column.)
- **Chain decomposition of E's ripple on the episodes (V280 rev 2 cells, open loop):** amp(32·sp) 890–1910 (the setpoint ripple openpilot's
  command puts in) vs amp(fb) 1200–2500 (the rate-feedback ripple), nearly in phase, so E = 32sp − fb is their difference (corr(E, −fb)
  0.54–0.84, corr(E, 32sp) 0.02–0.52). **Command frozen (1 Hz LPF) → T_sim ripple ×0.93–1.53 (larger); rate ripple removed → ×0.76–1.42.**
  openpilot's ripple currently follows the wheel and partly cancels the fb term — a statement about the path, not closed-loop stability.
- **The chain reproduces the tap moderately**: T_sim vs T_meas corr 0.45–0.81 (V280 map; 0.43–0.74 under rev 3's), slope 0.5–0.9, sim leads
  by 30–64°; T_sim ∠+65…+94° re rate vs T_meas ∠+4…+59°. BELIEF: ~15–20 ms of unmodelled delivery lag plus the 50 Hz tap's sample-hold.
- **The torsion bar rings**: 0x18F driver torque 4–12 Hz amplitude 280–915 raw, coh 1.00, lagging the rate by 122–151° (column twist), hands
  light (p50 140–300 raw) — as servo_at_reference.py found on r31.

**Verdict (BELIEF, strong, by elimination under the chain model; the EVIDENCE lines are above):** the lane-change ring is a **closed-loop
mode of the OUTER loop — openpilot ↔ EPS — at the column's ~7.5–8 Hz resonance**, not an EPS-internal limit cycle and not a clamp/rail.
(1) The command carries the line at coherence 1.00 and openpilot's block is live at 7.8 Hz with gain ~700–900 counts/deg; (2) that block and
its tune are unchanged between the smooth (V112, stock) and the oscillating drives; (3) between V112 and V280 rev 2 the only EPS change that
acts in this regime is the **map: V280's line gives 4.3 Y/idx everywhere vs stock's 2–2.25 below idx 24 and 1.2–1.5 at idx 24–64 — 1.9× V112's
cmd→setpoint gain at idx 12 and 2.9–3.6× at idx 24–58 (orchestrator's numbers), exactly where these lane changes live (idx pk 27–52)** —
while the inner rate loop's own gain (fb → Kp) is untouched by the map and is lower on these drives (idx p50 4–5 → Kp ~265 vs V112's idx 8);
no P rail, fb clamp, D rail or sum rail is active (all 0.000–0.009); (4) V112 at matched speed and |cmd| shows none of it (4–8 Hz 7–10 wire² vs
210–320; 0 episodes in 78 s; its 22 excursions ring at 0.7–2.7 deg/s on a different 10–11 Hz residual). The operator's "LKAS PID loop" is right
about the knob (the EPS's command-to-rate gain); "openpilot is not in the loop" is not supported — its command is the 7.8 Hz carrier.

## 6. What this licenses (BELIEF unless marked)
- **The lever is the low-/mid-idx map slope (the cmd→setpoint gain openpilot sees), not clamps, not Kp's rail, not the fb clamp** — none is
  active (EVIDENCE). V280 rev 2's line puts 4.3 Y/idx at idx 12–58 (1.9–3.6× V112); rev 3's ×2 put 4–4.5 there. A map back at stock slope
  below idx ~64 with the raised top kept would halve-to-third the outer gain where lane changes live and leave the high-angle top alone.
- **openpilot-side test, no flash**: halve SteerKP (the only live kp knob, 0.6 on all routes) or low-pass the command; the sentence a null
  licenses: *"if the 7.8 Hz lane-change line survives a halved SteerKP at ≥ 25 m/s during laneChangeState ≠ off windows, the outer loop is
  not the exciter and this verdict is wrong."* The instrument is already on the wire (laneChangeState + cmd + rate + tap).
- **Score it per lane change**, not per drive: six of six hands-light lane changes ≥ 17.7 m/s rang on r32/r33; the readout is the 4–12 Hz rate
  amplitude and the cmd coherence at f0 inside each laneChangeState window (`lanechange_windows.py`).
