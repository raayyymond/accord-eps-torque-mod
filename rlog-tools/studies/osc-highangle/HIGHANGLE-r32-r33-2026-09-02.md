**BELIEF — what this means for the lever.** The rev 2 map did what it was built to do on the stalled class: the wheel now reaches 93 % of its
reference hands-light, the idx ≥ 200 frames carry ripple/level 0.03, and the stalled-P-railed stutter is gone from the log. The ripple the
operator still feels in strong turns is the same 7 Hz loop mode, now expressed where the servo sits at its crossover with P linear — mid-command
(idx 26–173), 41–91 deg/s, 2–8 m/s. Pinning P further (a higher top) would only move the crossover again, since the wheel follows the reference;
the open-loop chain says Kd = 0 changes the ripple ≤ 0.04 and Kp × 0.5 RAISES ripple/level (0.44 → 0.57, 0.61 → 0.75 …) because it cuts the push
faster than the ripple. What cuts a crossover limit cycle is the LOOP gain at 7 Hz through P — which no open-loop counterfactual on these frames
can size (the rate ripple is the closed loop's own product). Candidates, in order: the Kp LERP at mid idx (68–136, where these episodes live) and
the D term as phase lead — both need the closed-loop question answered by a drive, not by this chain. The override cliff is grazed 2–11 % of frames
and is NOT the mechanism here (it was not on r31 either).
