## 🛑 Decoder defect found on the way (EVIDENCE, r31 segment 6, raw bytes)
`probe/decode_v278r3_torque_tap.py` decodes CAN 427 as `((b0&0x7F)<<3)|(b1>>5)` (DBC Motorola layout). On this wire
byte 0 is ONLY ever `0x80`/`0x82` and byte 1 spans 0–255, so that formula reads 0–22 and never sets the sign bit.
The kit's other convention `((b0&3)<<8)|b1` (direct_read_v276.py) gives a field with max 673, sign bit on 54 % of
frames, r = +0.54 signed / +0.67 magnitude against the 0xE4 command; a 15-window bit sweep confirms bits 6–15 of the
frame are the only 10-bit window that tracks the command. **Any V278r3/V279r2 numbers produced by the probe decoder
(saturation duty, damping duty, T-vs-cmd slope) are of the wrong bits.** This study uses `((b0&3)<<8)|b1`.

## Ranked diagnosis
1. **(b′) The lane is IN the 7 Hz loop, and the 7 Hz line is the kit's known ratchet mode, now fired by the ×2 map at
   high demand.** EVIDENCE: T oscillates at exactly the rate's f_dom with coherence 1.00 in every F7 episode; |T| level in
   the >60° bins is 8× the low-angle level (736–744 vs 88 p50). BELIEF: 7.0–7.6 Hz is the kit's ~7.8 Hz "loop-generated,
   gain-driven line, absent on stock" (memories `accord-the-8hz-mode-is-the-loop-not-the-plant`,
   `accord-ratchet-is-a-gain-driven-line`); the ×2 reference at a railed command doubles the delivered level and eats the
   1.2–1.6× gain margin that memory records. The measured T-vs-rate phase (+80°, T leading) is closer to quadrature than
   to pumping (0°) or damping (180°); whether the in-phase residue (cos 80° ≈ 0.17) is pumping depends on the wire sign
   convention of T vs the 0x18F rate, which this study did NOT re-derive — **needs the sign chain (adv278r3b's) applied
   to `((b0&3)<<8)|b1`, not to the DBC decode.**
2. **(a) Rail at the map ceiling — PARTLY.** EVIDENCE: 9 of 10 F7 episodes sit at idx 237–238 (cmd 4096) — the setpoint is
   at the ceiling — but T never approaches the rail (sat 0.000, |T| p90 ≤ 1300 vs 2472), and four F7 episodes run at
   idx 112–158. The ceiling is the usual setting, not the mechanism; "P rails, T at 310" is FALSE on this drive.
3. **(e) Angle-dependent base assist — NOT SEPARABLE HERE.** Every F7 episode is at 3–9 m/s in a turn with the driver's
   hand on (1200–1370 raw); no hands-off high-angle stretch exists on r31 to split it. What separates: a hands-off
   high-angle stretch (tq < 500) with LKAS engaged, or one F7-style turn on a K=1 (stock-map) image with this tap.
4. **(d) Override cliff — NO for F7, YES for F2.** F7 driver torque is 900–1000 raw below the first knot. F2 (3 episodes,
   2.3–2.9 Hz) sits on the cliff and exists on stock too.
5. **(c) openpilot's command oscillating — NO.** cmd is railed at 4096 in 7 of 10 F7 episodes (band amp 1–189 of 4096),
   desired-lateral-accel swing 0.01–0.04 vs error swing 0.06–0.9: the planner is flat, the error swings because the car
   does. The 7 Hz is inside the EPS loop.
