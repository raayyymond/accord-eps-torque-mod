## 1. ATTRIBUTION FROM THE TAP — all three routes ARE V283 [EVIDENCE]

Three independent instruments, none of which reads the build label.

### 1a. Ki fitted from the 427 delivered-torque tap

The integrator is in series with the tap, so in the unclamped regime the chain is **affine in Ki**:

```
T_meas - T_sim(Ki=0)  =  Ki * [ -(254/256)(5346/32768) * lag(J) ],    J = the Ki=1 accumulator
                                                        (J = cumsum(deadband(E>>5, 4))/8/128, cleared at disengage)
```

so **the regression slope IS Ki (0xC63E6)**. Windows are de-meaned *locally* — an open-loop replay of an
accumulator drifts in its absolute level but not in its slope, and that drift is exactly what defeats a
whole-route fit (the naive whole-route slope is attenuation-biased to ~6–13).

## 4. In one paragraph: what the integrator did to the car

**Ki 50 did precisely the job it was cut for, and its cost is not the one the prereg feared.** It removed
the P-only deadband: the seven stalled-wheel runs of r35 (14.8 s, wheel at 36 % of the reference at idx
40–80) became **one 1.4 s run across all three routes**, delivery at idx 20–40 rose **1.75×** and at idx
40–80 to **87 %** of the map's reference, and the dead-frame fraction fell from **0.336 to 0.048** — all
without waking the 7 Hz ring (F7 0.0 per 100 s on all three, tap ripple/level 0.117 vs r35's 0.18) or the
20 Hz creep line (bar 17.7 vs 19.2 raw). The integral term is doing real work — it is **68 % of |I|+|P|+|D|**
and rails at its ±10240 anti-windup clamp on 1.1–2.9 % of engaged frames. **The consequence is the
operator's oversteer, and it is arithmetic, not a defect:** the loop now delivers what the map asks, while
openpilot is still asking through a SteerRatio of 12.5 against a car whose real ratio is ~16.1 — so a
request that used to arrive at 0.53–0.62 of its size now arrives at 0.70–0.87, and in tight curves the road
curvature went from 0.95× asked to 1.03–1.13× asked. **V281 rev 3's understeer and V283's oversteer are the
same 12.5 bias seen through a loop that under-delivered and then stopped under-delivering.** The one
genuinely new and unwanted behaviour on the wire is §3b: **the accumulator does not clear at disengage** —
V283 keeps delivering 150–383 tap counts for 0.5–1.0 s after `STEER_REQUEST` drops, where both Ki-0 builds
are at the noise floor within 0.5 s.

**On the operator's own reasoning** — *"an integrator on steering angle rate is just steering angle, which
would NOT be used in a PID loop on angular acceleration"* — the wire agrees with the premise and does not
settle the conclusion: this integrator does integrate a **rate** error, so its state is an angle-like
quantity, and it is now the dominant term in the sum (0.68 share). Whether that is the wrong device or the
right device pointed at the wrong reference is a design question, not a measurement; what the measurement
says is that **the deadband it was cut to fix is fixed, and the remaining road-level error is the steering
ratio, which is a one-parameter fix outside the ECU.**

| E4 — rise time, first 0.5 s of a held error | 0.5 s | **77.9** ✗ | **51.5** ✗ | (n=3) | (n=2) | (n=4) | **NO — BROKEN** |

**The two estimators that are decisive AND pass their controls both say 50**, and they agree with the byte
(0xC63E6 = 0x32 = 50, verified from the images by the orchestrator).

**Why the low readings arise — diagnosed, not asserted.** The accumulator is **leak-free**, so the error in
any open-loop reconstruction integrates without bound; every long-horizon estimator is drift-dominated.

- **E3 returns Ki = 0 on r36 and r38** — reported against my own conclusion. It is *uninformative*, not a
  vote for zero: on the Ki-0 controls the SSE at Ki 20 is **267× and 509×** the minimum; on r36 it is
  **1.077×**. The likelihood surface is ~250× flatter exactly where a real integrator lives. **E3b is the
  controlled test:** chunk the *same* estimator and it snaps to 50–55 on the V283 routes while the controls
  stay pinned at 0 at every chunk length. That is the drift diagnosis confirmed, not argued.
- **E1 is not reproducible across implementations** — 0.250/0.216/0.032 here, 0.65–0.76 as quoted by the
  orchestrator, different again from a third agent. The nulls agree (−0.001 vs −0.01), so the *sign* is
  solid and the *magnitude* is not. Dropping clamp-bound frames barely moves it (0.218/0.214/0.032), so the
  anti-windup clamp is not the cause — the drift is. The modelled accumulator rails on only 0.5–2.0 % of
  engaged frames and the sum clamp never binds.
- **E4 is broken and its output must not be used.** It reads **Ki_hat 51.5 on r35 and 77.9 on r34, both
  Ki-0 builds.** At the *onset* of a held error the P term is itself ramping, so d|T|/dt is dominated by
  dP/dt and E4 charges all of it to the integrator. It is kept in the script clearly marked, because
  **without the Ki-0 controls it would have "confirmed" ~50 and been believed.**

> **No physical mechanism was found that would lower the effective Ki below the cal.** A below-50 wire
> estimate is measurement error, not the ECU behaving as though Ki were 20. **Do not size a reduced dose
> off it.** If a smaller dose is wanted, size it from the *effect* — delivery at 87 % of the map's
> reference (§3c) and tight-curve tracking at 1.03–1.13× asked (§3d) — never from a fitted Ki.
