## 1. State (one-paragraph)

The EME snap = **three redundant authority-gate state machines** in `s_motor_torque_rate_shaper` (`FUN_00042af8`), OR-linked by a 3-way MIN, each a Q15 gate on the LKAS demand (node `0`=cut, instant; recovery ramps). They arm when LKAS demand exceeds **`tp+0x7422`=`0xC6422`=16384 (50% Q15)** — stock sits just below it, so **the EME is 2×-only**. Full-command 2× *authority* and the EME are the same thing. **Safe 2× = setpoint-gain build** (keeps demand under the gate). **High-end 2× = rescale the SM arming thresholds** (loosening; not fully pinned). See the verified memory for all addresses.
